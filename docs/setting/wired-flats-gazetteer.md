THE WIRED — THE FLATS
Matrix Gazetteer & Node Key
Companion to the canon map "The Wired — The Flats." A hacker's field guide to the public matrix-scape of the Ossian Reach underlevels: every district master node, corporate fortress, and street-level joint you can jack into during downtime projects or pre-run legwork.

How to read this gazetteer
Every node carries an Echelon (E1–E4) security rating. This is the intrusion grade of the node's ICE, not the tier of the people who run it.
Echelon
Security Posture
What a runner faces
E1
Open / civic
Public master nodes, street joints. Minimal ICE, mostly logging. Anyone can knock.
E2
Guarded
Standard commercial ICE, watchdog programs, alert-on-tamper. The working grade of the Flats.
E3
Hardened
Layered ICE, active trace, gray/black hybrids. Corp-adjacent, gang-treasury, deep-net fringes.
E4
Lethal
Black ICE. Kills or lobotomizes intruders. Corp fortresses and the Grid uplink. Do not knock without a plan.

The vertical rule (canon): The Flats are the low-security floor of the Wired. The Grid sits above, behind the Black ICE Barrier — a single guarded uplink chokepoint, all E4. Everything on this map is Flats-native (E1–E3) except the corporate fortress-nodes and the Grid gate, which are E4.
Canon note (districts): The Ossian Reach Handbook fixes ten canon districts. The matrix map renders those ten plus two matrix-only extensions — Cinderhold (the wasteland-gate settlement's uplink, canon as a place, here given a Flats-facing public node) and The Undernet (the deep-net boundary shading toward the Sinks). Both are flagged below.

I. THE GRID & THE BARRIER
THE GRID — Upper System
Echelon: E4 (Black ICE)
Owner: The Conglomerates, jointly; policed by Meridian traffic-net and corp Sanctums.
Vibe: A cathedral ceiling of cold, ordered light above the reef. Clean geometry, no clutter, everything watched. The architecture itself is a threat display.
What to score: Licensed paydata, corp memos, clean SIN-flags, Grid-warden favors — none of it cheap, all of it lethal to reach.
Plot hook: The Climb. A Flats crew has to get a payload up past the Barrier and back down without tripping the boundary scan. Warden Krael "the Landlord" will sell an uplink window — for a price that's never just nuyen.
BLACK ICE BARRIER & Guarded Uplink Chokepoint
Echelon: E4 (Black ICE)
Owner: Automated corp defense grid; no single hand on it.
Vibe: A wall of adaptive lethal light with one narrow, watched door punched through it. Everything funnels to the chokepoint; everything at the chokepoint is seen.
What to score: The window itself — a timed gap in the trace. That's the whole heist.
Plot hook: One Way Up, Many Watching. The uplink only opens for authenticated traffic. Someone has to be the authenticated traffic. Who's the crew willing to burn to be the key?

II. DISTRICT MASTER NODES
The public civic node for each district — the on-ramp every resident and runner uses. Master nodes are deliberately open (E1) so the district functions; the real security lives in what they connect to.
THE SWITCHBOARD — Master Node (canon 01)
Echelon: E1 (the on-ramp) — but Cassavir's private layer runs E3.
Owner: Nominally civic; functionally Mama Cassavir, "the Switchboard."
Vibe: A dead rail switching-station wired back to life, humming with a hundred routed deals. The seam between up and down.
What to score: Work. Every hook in the Reach can plausibly route through here.
Plot hook: The On-Ramp. The default first job. Cassavir hands the crew a contract — and a relationship to spend and endanger.
THE STACKS — Master Node (canon 03)
Echelon: E1
Owner: Contested — the Ninth Ward Kings hold the plaza; Ferrum's Rent Office holds the paper.
Vibe: Regimented company housing gone to seed. A rigid grid the Grid forgot.
What to score: Residency records, gang-parliament schedules, the Sparrows' child-courier routes.
Plot hook: The Rent War. Ferrum wants to "renovate" (evict) eight blocks. The Kings say no. The crew is hired by one side and courted by the other.
NEON SHAMBLES — Master Node (canon 02)
Echelon: E2 (dense commercial traffic = more watchdogs)
Owner: The market itself; the Kings tax it, Cassavir brokers its peace.
Vibe: Forty stories of stacked signage over a medieval tangle of stall-choked lanes. The Flats' beating night-market heart.
What to score: Anything for sale, the Deadletter job board, simsense chip-schematics.
Plot hook: The Long Bazaar Steal. A one-of-a-kind chip surfaces at a stall for one night. Every faction wants it. So does the crew's Johnson.
GLASSHOOK — Master Node (canon 08)
Echelon: E2
Owner: No one holds the Fallen Atrium cleanly; it's a rotating gang court.
Vibe: A collapsed retail arcology gone feral — a vertical mall turned sideways, shopfronts turned squats.
What to score: Missing-persons intel (Lost & Found), fenced luxury goods, pirate-media broadcast access.
Plot hook: Lost & Found. The body-trade broker has a client's face on file. The client wants it erased. The face belongs to someone the crew knows.
SLACKWATER — Master Node (canon 04)
Echelon: E2
Owner: Nyx smuggling interests; Ferrum runs the licensed cranes.
Vibe: Where the Flats meet the cistern-sea. Finger-piers, sunken warehouses, smuggler channels down to the Sinks.
What to score: Smuggling manifests, Sinkmouth access codes, contraband cargo tags.
Plot hook: The Sinkmouth Opens. Something is coming up the sealed channel from the Sinks. Nyx wants it moved quietly. It should not be moved at all.
THE INTERCHANGE — Master Node (canon 05)
Echelon: E2
Owner: Wheelman collectives; Ironclad runs the Weigh Station checkpoint.
Vibe: A cathedral of on-ramps — stacked freeways, sunken lots, the fastest way through the Flats.
What to score: Convoy schedules, Ironclad checkpoint blind-spots, rig-runner staging intel.
Plot hook: The Clean Route Survey. Kestrel Dynamics is mapping the Interchange for autonomous transit. The wheelmen see the end of their world in it.
WIRESIDE — Master Node (canon 09)
Echelon: E3 (data slum — everything here is wired deep)
Owner: Technomancer collectives; Meridian sub-corp watches the fringe.
Vibe: Where the Wired comes down to the street. Server-shanties, cooling-mist alleys, deep-net divers.
What to score: Deep-net access, net-runners for hire, stolen bandwidth, the Ghost Node's secrets.
Plot hook: The Ghost Node. A haunted deep-net address answers when it shouldn't. The technomancers are afraid of it. It knows the crew's names.
CINDER MARKET — Master Node (canon 06)
Echelon: E2
Owner: The Wrench guild and the Kiln gang share uneasy ground.
Vibe: The Flats' foundry-quarter. Furnace-lit workshops, drone-forges, the best off-books tech south of the Grid.
What to score: Chrome, drone frames, combat-frame refits, fabrication schematics.
Plot hook: The Piston Yard Prototype. A combat-frame refit is too good — someone leaked Ironclad milspec into the Flats. Ironclad wants it back, quietly.
THE SPILLWAY — Master Node (canon 07)
Echelon: E2
Owner: The Undertow drain-gang (Culvert Kings); Nyx controls the Sluice Gate.
Vibe: Built in and over the hive's storm-drains. Floods on schedule, hides what the water takes.
What to score: Smuggler-highway timing, salvage sieved from the drains, flood-schedule data.
Plot hook: What the Water Takes. The next scheduled flood will bury evidence — or a witness. The crew has until the sluice opens.
GALLOWS END — Master Node (canon 10)
Echelon: E3 (edge district — dead-air creep raises the grade)
Owner: The Last Light boss; the Lamp on Ninth kin keep the Vigil Shrine.
Vibe: The bottom of the Flats, where the floor gives out. The last lit blocks before the Warrens swallow the light.
What to score: Warrens-salvage, descent gear, SIN-vanishing services (the Deadline), Warrens intel.
Plot hook: The Deadline. A SIN the crew needs erased is at the Deadline broker — but so is a runner who paid to disappear and shouldn't have been let to.
CINDERHOLD — Master Node (matrix extension; canon place, Ch. 6)
Echelon: E3 (wasteland gate — thin signal, hardened by necessity)
Owner: Marrow, the Ranger-captain; Cassavir's colder twin switchboard.
Vibe: The free settlement at the wasteland gate, uplinked to the Flats by a single hardened line. The seam where the hive hands off to the wastes.
What to score: Wasteland caravan routes, off-books Verdant supply intel, early-warning on the dead-air spread.
Plot hook: The Clean Route Clock. Kestrel wants to civilize the gate. Marrow knows the corridor runs through ground that's going quiet. He needs deniable eyes — the crew's.
THE UNDERNET — Master Node (matrix extension; the deep-net boundary)
Echelon: E4 (dead-air fringe — the Wired thins toward the Quiet Floor)
Owner: No one living. The Ferryman tollkeeps the approach; something below may answer to him.
Vibe: Where the Wired stops reaching. Signal thins, then thought thins. The matrix-shadow of Deadfall Nine.
What to score: Nothing safely. Lost paydata, dead runners' last uploads, and truths the hive walled off.
Plot hook: The Quiet Floor Leaks. Nodes just above the Undernet are going dark one by one. The pattern is climbing toward the Flats. Someone has to go look.

III. CORPORATE FORTRESS-NODES
Corp presence pushed down into the Flats' reef. All E4 — Black ICE, no exceptions. These are not joints you walk into; they are walls you plan against.
CADUCEUS — Med-Corp Fortress
Echelon: E4
Owner: Caduceus (the med-corp); runs the Flats' deniable clinics through cut-outs.
Vibe: A sterile pink-lit vault-node bleeding into the Grafthouse ripperdoc trade below it. Healing as a leash.
What to score: Patient records, cyber-limb serials, the deniable link between Caduceus and the back-alley docs.
Plot hook: Deniable Care. A Grafthouse ripperdoc is installing Caduceus-tagged chrome with a backdoor. Someone's harvesting the patients' senses. Prove it and you own the med-corp.
ZIGGURAT TRUST — Bank Vault-Node
Echelon: E4
Owner: Ziggurat Trust (finance conglomerate).
Vibe: A gold-cold monolith of certified credit, the one place in the Flats where nuyen is real and watched.
What to score: Account trails, laundered-credstick provenance, the ledger of who really owns the Flats.
Plot hook: Following the Nuyen. A run's payout traces back through Ziggurat to a name that shouldn't be funding street work. The bank would kill to keep that quiet.
OMNIWAVE — Media Spire
Echelon: E4
Owner: Omniwave / the Media Conglomerate.
Vibe: A blazing orange broadcast tower flooding the reef with the manufactured calm. Reality, as edited.
What to score: Un-aired footage, the edit-room truth, blackmail cuts, pirate-broadcast override keys.
Plot hook: The Cut They Buried. Omniwave has footage that ends a corp war — or starts one. Both sides hire the crew to get it. Only one payload exists.
IRONCLAD — Arms Grid
Echelon: E4
Owner: Ironclad (arms conglomerate); runs cage-fight talent scouting and convoy security in the Flats.
Vibe: A red katana-marked fortress-node, all edges. The hand that arms every side and profits from all of them.
What to score: Weapon serials, convoy routes, milspec schematics, the buyer list.
Plot hook: The Leaked Milspec. Ironclad hardware surfaced in the Cinder Market (see Piston Yard). Ironclad wants the leak plugged. The leaker wants to live.
NIGHTMARKET — Data-Haven
Echelon: E4 (fortress-grade, but criminal black ICE — trapped, not corporate)
Owner: The Blacknet syndicate (canon "Blacknet Market" node).
Vibe: A poison-green data-haven where anything can be bought if you survive the buying. The anti-Ziggurat.
What to score: Stolen paydata, zero-day exploits, the auction block, identities.
Plot hook: The Blacknet Auction. The crew's own stolen data is on the block — or their SIN is. The auction closes at dawn.

V. RUNNING THE MATRIX-SCAPE (GM Notes)
Downtime dives. Between runs, a hacker can jack into any E1–E2 node for legwork: buy intel, scout a target's district, line up a fence, or fish the job boards (Deadletter Post, the master nodes). Treat as a downtime project — an easy test at E1, harder at E2.
Pre-run legwork. Before hitting a district physically, dive its master node to map POIs, spot ICE, and find a back door. The map is the recon.
Echelon = the wall. E3 nodes want a real plan; E4 nodes want a heist. The corp fortresses and the Grid uplink should never be a casual dive — they're campaign obstacles, not errands.
The vertical is the obstacle (canon). Getting from a Flats E1 node up through the E4 Barrier into the Grid is the shape of a great run. Cassavir knows the grey ways; the Barrier knows everyone else.
The dead-air climbs. The Undernet and Deeptrace sit at the seam where the street tier touches the cosmic one (Deadfall Nine). A crew that keeps diving deep stops doing street runs and starts touching the war.

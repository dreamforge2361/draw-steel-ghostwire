# NPCs of the Reach

*The Humanoid Adversaries & Civilians of the Flats*

The named and the nameless who stand between the crew and the job. This roster collects the humanoid adversaries and civilians of the Flats — the gangers a fresh crew actually fights, the corp security who answer the alarm, the specialists and fixers a crew hires or fears, and the ordinary people who make the Flats a place and not a battlefield.

## Mid-Boss Threats

The named danger at the top of a Flats run — the bosses a crew plans around.

### Gangers

#### The Warlord

*the boss who owns the district*

*Stats: see the Ghostwire Bestiary.*

**Hook.** Not the strongest fighter in the Flats — the most entrenched. Kill him in the open and the district fractures into a turf war by nightfall.

#### Razorline Prime

*the augmented duelist*

*Stats: see the Ghostwire Bestiary.*

**Hook.** The Warlord's personal enforcer and the reason challengers stop coming. Pure damage-and-tempo — no minions to manage, just a knife you can't reliably touch. Pair with the Warlord for the district's climax fight.

### Corporate Security

#### Corp Enforcer

*the specialist sent to close the file*

*Stats: see the Ghostwire Bestiary.*

**Hook.** When a corp decides the crew is a liability worth a name, this is the name. He is not defending a building — he is executing a contract, and he brought the whole escalation ladder with him. Killing him flags the crew for the conglomerate's next tier of response.

#### Ironclad Warden

*the black-site chief of security*

*Stats: see the Ghostwire Bestiary.*

**Hook.** The Ironclad trainer, promoted to run a site the corp does not admit exists. A defensive anchor: he turns a room into a fortress and buys time for the Villain-tier escalation. Flank him or the fight never ends.

### Specialists & Fixers

#### The Ripper

*the black-clinic butcher-surgeon*

*Stats: see the Ghostwire Bestiary.*

**Hook.** He keeps the Warlord's soldiers on their feet and installs the district's illegal chrome. A support boss: leave him alive and the enemy side never runs out of fighters. The clinics whisper his street name; nobody uses his real one.

#### Null-Prophet

*the rogue technomancer running the node*

*Stats: see the Ghostwire Bestiary.*

**Hook.** The mind the crew is really fighting when they jack into a node it has claimed. A boss-tier escalation of the Corp Netrunner.

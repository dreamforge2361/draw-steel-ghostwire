## The Power Roll

When a runner acts under pressure, they make a **Power Roll**: roll two ten-sided dice and add a characteristic (and any bonuses). The total lands in one of three **results**:

| Result | Total |
|---|---|
| **Low** | 11 or less |
| **Middle** | 12–16 |
| **High** | 17 or more |

Ghostwire always prints results in this order — low, middle, high — with the weakest effect first.

**“Improve the outcome by one result”** means low becomes middle, or middle becomes high.

## Ghostwire modifiers

### Connection states

Your connection to the Wired changes your rolls (`21-the-wire.md`):

| State | Wired Power Rolls | Real-world Power Rolls |
|---|---|---|
| Disconnected | — | — |
| **Overlay** | — | **Bane** |
| **Jacked In** | **Edge** | Not allowed — your body is inert |

A Wired roll is any Power Roll for a Matrix Verb, Program, or other ability with the **Wired** keyword.

### The Hacking skill

Having the **Hacking** skill gives an **edge** on every rolling Matrix Verb and other Wired Power Roll. For those rolls it replaces the usual skill benefit.

### Gear and armor

- **Encumbrance:** medium and heavy armor impose banes on some tests (`08`).
- **Mods** can add edges in specific situations; installed Wired software can add edges to named rolls (`10`, `19`).
- **Chrome** grants situational edges; a **Damaged** implant works at a penalty (`09`).

### Result wording in Ghostwire text

Abilities, perks, and gear print their effects by result. Where a Wired ability says **low** “works, but something goes wrong,” that usually means Trace Alert rises (`21`).

## Chrome and Body Integrity

Since **B44b**, three heroes carry chrome and five do not. Barak spends **9** Body Integrity (Cyberlimb Arm 5 +
Dermal Plating 4), Kessic **3** (Datajack 1 + Cyber-Eyes 2) and Vira **1** (Datajack); everyone else spends **0**.
All are legal against the 20 cap. Krow is a Cyborg and uses Frame Modules rather than Body Integrity at all; the three
Veil casters stay unchromed because the shipped chrome pack has **no Soft-grade SKUs** to give them
(`docs/raw/09-chrome-body-integrity.md`, and see `LOADOUTS.md`).

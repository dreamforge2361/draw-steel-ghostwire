# Pregen build notes — assumptions and judgement calls (B44 · B44b)

Generated by `tools/pregens-to-actors.mjs` (Actors) and `tools/pregen-stories-to-journals.mjs` (fiction).
Re-run both, then `node tools/build-packs.mjs` with Foundry closed, after any change here.

## The Tier 5 problem (read this first)

The dossier PDF ships each hero as a **Tier 5** sheet: Stamina like `20 (Tier-5 Base) + Physique 4 × 2`, characteristic
profiles of 4 / 3 / 2, and Tier-5 ability riders. The spike says to discard that math and build at **Level 1**, but it
also says to "take dossier numbers when present" for characteristics. Those two instructions collide, because the
dossier's characteristic numbers *are* Tier-5 numbers — a 4 is not a legal Level 1 score.

**What I did:** kept the dossier's *shape* and dropped its *scale*. Each hero uses the Draw Steel Level 1 spread
**2 / 2 / 1 / 1 / 0**, with the 2s on the class's own core characteristics, which in every case matches which
characteristics the dossier had highest. So Krow is still a Physique/Reflex bruiser and Kessic is still a Logic deckhead,
at Level 1 values. **If you wanted the dossier's literal numbers, this is the decision to overturn** — say so and I
will rebuild.

## Per-hero assumptions

- **Backgrounds and professions** are not in the dossiers; I picked the closest shipped Background/Profession to each
  origin story: Vessa faith-district/acolyte · Kaïs corp-arcology/entertainer (he comes down off the Glass Tier) ·
  Krow industrial-deep/security-guard · Barak sprawl-district/fixer · Wren transit-hub/courier ·
  Sabbat undercity-barrens/deck-jockey · Vira undercity-barrens/rig-tech · Kessic sprawl-district/deck-jockey.
- **Kits** are the closest shipped Kit to the dossier's loadout, constrained by each class's own kit pool. **Sabbat**
  takes the light **no-kit** option, which is what the Technomancer chapter expects of a caster with a light kit slot.
- **B44b corrected two kits.** **Barak** was on Juggernaut, which is **not in the Commander's kit pool** (that pool has
  no heavy kits) — he is now on **Saturation**, and his Stamina moved 30 → 24. **Kessic** was on the caster no-kit
  placeholder and ended up holding two kit items; he is now on the **Nyx “Switchblade”** decker kit, and still carries
  his Street Deck.
- **Ancestry traits** are a representative legal pick, not the dossier's (the dossiers predate the current trait
  packages). Krow's **Arcane Severance** is mandatory rather than chosen. Wren and Vira carry **Changer Forms** plus
  the locked lineage trait — **Raven** for Wren, **Rat** for Vira, per the spike and Changer B6c.
- **Skills** are four per hero, chosen to match the story and the class's own skill grants.
- **Abilities**: three per hero — the class signature(s) plus a subclass feature — so a sheet has something usable
  the moment it opens. They are copies of the shipped ability Items, so their numbers stay whatever the packs say.
- **Biographies** are two-sentence dossier blurbs written from the anthology's own descriptions. The full origin story
  lives in the fiction journal, not on the sheet, as the spike asks.

## Chrome

No pregen spends Body Integrity: all eight start at **BI 20** with no implants, which is legal under Pass A and leaves
chroming to the player. Krow, as a Cyborg, does not use Body Integrity at all.

## Art

All eight portraits extracted cleanly from the PDF (see `ART-NOTES.md`): 800×800 PNGs with transparency, one per hero,
used as both actor `img` and `prototypeToken.texture.src`. No placeholder was needed.

## Deliberately not done

- **No Medic pregen** — the roster is eight and the spike forbids inventing one.
- **No advancement past Level 1**, no invented subclasses, no edits to `docs/raw/`.
- The source PDF stays **untracked** (`.gitignore` ignores `docs/masters/pregens/*.pdf`); the text extract, stories,
  portraits and these notes are committable.

## Ghostwire canon note — lineages and forms (2026-09-16)

Lineages and forms are **no longer fiction-only** for Ghostwire. The Dragon Knight chassis (Beast-Hide ← Wyrmplate, 3 ancestry points, six purchased traits, all numbers) is unchanged; lineage and form add these locked effects and nothing else:

- **Lineage** (pick one at creation, always on): Raven — Walk + Fly · Rat — size 1S · Wolf — +2 speed.
- **Forms** (maneuver to change; exactly one active; start in Human):
  - Human — edge on tests to pass as or blend in among baseline humans.
  - Hybrid — edge on Intimidation; melee free strikes deal +1 damage; can’t pass as human.
  - Beast — edge on Stealth and Perception; can’t use manufactured weapons; can’t speak clear language.

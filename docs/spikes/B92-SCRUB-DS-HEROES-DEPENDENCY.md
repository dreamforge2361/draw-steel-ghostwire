## Policy (LOCKED — Michael 2026-09-18/19, tightened same night)

### Play
Players do **not** need *Draw Steel Heroes* or any separate Draw Steel rulebook to play Ghostwire from this manuscript / PDF. Play = this book + dice + (optional) Foundry.

### Draw Steel naming (front-matter only)

**ONLY** these files may mention Draw Steel / MCDM / Creator License / that Ghostwire is a **derived product** on their engine:

- `docs/raw/00-front-matter.md`
- `docs/manuscript/00-front/title-page.md`
- `docs/manuscript/00-front/credits.md`

Front matter **must** include (once, clear): Ghostwire is an independent derived product using the Draw Steel engine under MCDM’s Creator License / open licensing terms; not affiliated with / not endorsed by MCDM. **Do not** say players must buy Heroes.

**Everywhere else** in player-facing print (`docs/raw/` chapters after front matter, lore, appendix, how-to-use body): **Ghostwire only**. No “Draw Steel”, no “Heroes” (the book), no “as DS…”, no §F / DS section pointers, no “use the official rules”, no “DS under the hood” in characteristics tables.

### Exceptions
- **Foundry software:** In Foundry sidebars may say enable **Draw Steel - Ghostwire Build** on a compatible Foundry world (module / system package). Once per sidebar max. Not a rules-book citation.
- **Internal spikes/masters:** design history OK.
- **B78 / B83** IP scrub still holds (no third-party cyberpunk / 40K / WoD name-checks).

### Rewrite rules
1. Complete the sentence in Ghostwire. If a procedure was only “as Draw Steel”, write a brief Ghostwire-original table procedure or point at another GW chapter (`03`, `04`, `24`, `26`).
2. Do **not** paste or reconstruct substantial MCDM prose.
3. Prefer “this book”, chapter pointers, or “Ghostwire”.

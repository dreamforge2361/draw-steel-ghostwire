## Rules baseline

- **Trade Cant** is everyone’s starting language: the working speech of the Reach. Other languages come from your Background, class, People, education, and perks.
- **Machine translation** carries simple communication, but a translator isn’t knowing the language: nuance, code phrases, ritual language, and deception slip through. The Director decides whether a negotiation, a read, or a deception works through one.
- **Knowledge-only languages** need real knowledge; translation gives a surface gloss at best. They are every Arcane & sacred tongue, Black-Site Code, every Machine & matrix tongue except Wire Speak, and the deep-lore tongues Void Cant, Infernal Speech, Old Liturgical, Dead Ops Tongue, and Extradimensional Technical.

Languages create access, secrecy, and flavor, not constant blockage: everyday scenes run in shared tongues, while archives, rites, black sites, and enclaves open to those who know the words.

## Language categories

Common urban · National / cultural · Corporate · Arcane & sacred · Machine & matrix · Ancestral (Peoples) · Extradimensional / dead / deep lore. See the master for each category’s languages.

**Charter Age / the Ten:** the founding Congress (~1930) seated ten corporations. Current play uses **the Twelve**. Historical flashbacks may say “the Ten.” Do not treat Ten as present-day roster truth.

## Usage
- First mention in a chapter: **Ticker** — Full Name (or Full Name (**Ticker**)).
- Thereafter: ticker alone is fine when context is clear.
- Sub-corps keep their names; parent ticker may prefix (e.g. FER Deepworks, AEQ Writ Inquest, LAZ White Door Flight).
- Never invent a thirteenth seat without Michael. Kestrel Dynamics remains the seatless climber.

## Locked sales & lifestyle rules

- **AEQ — Council-only.** Aequitas Mandate may sell security, investigation, and justice services **only to the Council**: the Corporate Congress, the Chair, or the pooled authority of the seated Conglomerates acting as a body. Forbidden clients: private parties, runners, gangs, fixers, and **any single mega acting alone**. A Chair-signed warrant or a Congress resolution is a client. An IRN-style private contract is not. Street slang: “the Mandate,” “AEQ seal,” “Council badge.”
- **AEQ is not** Ironclad Martial (open-market PMC), not HALO’s Aureole Security (private HALO army), and not Sanctum Assurance / the Grey Ledger (surveillance, records, private law and intel). AEQ is the Council’s exclusive sword.
- **LAZ — Lifestyle extract.** Paying a Lifestyle band’s upkeep (`docs/raw/26-lifestyle-downtime.md`) **includes** that band’s Lazarus contract tier. No separate ¥ line unless the Director adds a premium rider. Distinct from Caduceus Vitalis (clinics, pharma, chrome-flesh). Street slang: “Laz,” “white door,” “extract contract.”

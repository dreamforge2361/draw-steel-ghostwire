# Ghostwire Art Style Lock (from Core Sourcebook + Lore Book)

**Sources reviewed:** `GHOSTWIRE — Core Sourcebook.pdf` (cover, part openers, class pages), `GHOSTWIRE-Lore-Book-V2-FINAL.pdf` (cover, Wired overlay, Peoples plates).  
**Locked:** 2026-09-16 — use this brief for all future artwork generation / PDF art passes / Foundry journal art. Do not drift into generic bright cyberpunk, anime-clean, or painterly fantasy-only looks.

---

## One-sentence north star

**Cinematic cyberpunk-fantasy nocturne:** rain-soaked vertical megacity, neon in the gutters, and a pale “halo / spire / Signal” light above the clouds — grit and chrome below, almost sacred tech above.

---

## Palette

| Role | Colors |
|---|---|
| Base | Near-black, charcoal, wet asphalt, deep navy / steel blue |
| Neon accents | Hot pink / magenta, electric cyan, amber / orange signage |
| Apex / Signal | Warm gold / white-gold halo, soft god-ray beams through storm cloud |
| Flesh / undead | Desaturated pale skin, cool shadows (Revenants, ghosts) |
| UI / book chrome | Dark page grounds; cyan or gold hairlines; white sans titles |

Avoid: flat white daylight; candy pastels; pure green Matrix rain as the whole look; warm sunset orange dominating the frame.

---

## Lighting & atmosphere

- Night + heavy rain almost always; wet pavement mirrors neon.
- High contrast: deep shadows in the street; small hard neon punches; soft bloom on distant towers.
- Mist / steam / volumetric haze in urban canyons.
- Optional subtle **scan-line / holo overlay** on chapter plates (tech skin, not noisy VHS trash).
- Vertical rain streaks; rim light on hoods and coat edges from nearby orange work-lamps.

---

## Composition & world geometry

- **Extreme verticality** — street canyon → skybridges → clean upper spires → orbital/halo ring.
- Tiny human figures vs architecture (runners feel small).
- Recurring motifs: hooded coats, tactical packs with soft LED strips, drones/VTOLs with searchlights, kanji-like neon signage (setting flavor, not real-world brand logos), rusted gantries and dangling cables at street level.
- Upper city reads cleaner / whiter; lower city denser, darker, more signage clutter.
- Fantasy bleed is quiet, not D&D bright: ghosts, Veil light, undead pale faces, Wired lattice as glowing neural/circuit veil — always married to chrome and rain.

---

## Character / class plate look

- Dark tactical / street-fashion silhouette (trench, cloak, armor plates).
- Cyan visor / optic glow or pink iconography on gear as accent only.
- Action reads cinematic (Operator mid-burst, muzzle flash orange) but still grounded in rain and neon reflections.
- Peoples plates (e.g. Revenants): gothic-cyber — Victorian / tattered black coats + neon purple-blue streaks; undead beauty, not zombie gore comedy.

---

## Wired / Matrix imagery

- Meat face + **fine glowing lattice** (blue/pink neural-circuit overlay), eyes closed or soft.
- Dark industrial background; the overlay is delicate and dense, not chunky HUD chrome.

---

## Typography pairing (when generating page art / mock spreads)

- Titles: bold condensed all-caps sans, white.
- Section chips: small gold or cyan labels (`BOOK ONE · PART V`).
- Thin gold or cyan rules under titles.
- Body on dark grounds: light grey / white; cyan for rules-anchor callouts.

---

## Medium & finish

- Digital cinematic illustration / concept-art finish (not photo-bash collage, not flat vector).
- Painterly edges on rain and haze; sharper detail on foreground chrome and faces.
- Print-ready contrast: still readable when darkened for book backgrounds.

---

## Do / Don’t for generators

**Do:** rain, neon pink/cyan, gold halo/spire, vertical city, hooded runners from behind, wet reflections, drones, skybridges, subtle scanlines on plates.  
**Don’t:** sunny daytime sprawl; cartoon chibi; mud-brown grimdark-only palettes; generic high-fantasy pastoral; stock “hacker hoodie green code”; licensed corp logos; readable real brand text.

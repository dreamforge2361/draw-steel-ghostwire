# Ghostwire Core Rulebook — Chapter: Perks

**Status:** v1 draft (2026-09-16). Foundry: **Ghostwire Perks** compendium (pack `perks`).
**Source of record for v1 numbers:** the Foundry pack (`src/packs/perks/`). This chapter explains the system; the pack holds each perk’s exact text.
**Draw Steel spine:** the 47 Draw Steel perks, same mechanics, reskinned for the Reach.

## What a perk is

A perk is a trick of the trade: something you picked up on the job that has nothing to do with your class. A perk isn’t chrome, gear, or doctrine. It costs no ¥ and no Body Integrity. It’s training and experience, the way a Kit is.

Most perks work outside combat: talking your way past a checkpoint, jury-rigging a dead lift, knowing which fixer has the answer. A few give you a small maneuver or triggered action.

## The six perk types

Every perk has one type. Five types line up with how Ghostwire skills get used; the sixth is the weird stuff.

| Perk type | What it covers | Skills it leans on |
|---|---|---|
| **Crafting** | Building, modding, repairing, projects | Technical skills (Repair, Electronics, Engineering, Cybertech, …) |
| **Exploration** | Moving through the Reach, physical feats, montage work | Action skills (Athletics, Acrobatics, Perception, Survival, …) |
| **Interpersonal** | Talking, reading, and moving people | Social skills (Persuasion, Deception, Insight, Performance, …) |
| **Intrigue** | Stealth, disguise, cuffs, countermeasures, the street network | Stealth, Deception, Security Systems, Perception, Demolitions |
| **Lore** | Knowing things and finding out | Knowledge skills (Streetwise, Corporate, History, Matrix Theory, …) |
| **Supernatural** | Veil, Signal, and Wired-adjacent street weird | No skill needed |

Supernatural perks aren’t spellcasting. They’re small, strange gifts that show up on the street: a Veil-spirit living in an alley rat, a one-way whisper through the Signal, a flash of the emotion stuck to a murder weapon. Any hero can take one, but some tables will want a reason. A Cyborg can’t use a perk ability with the Magic keyword (Veil Trick) under Arcane Severance.

## When you gain perks

Classes grant perks as they level. A grant either lists the perk types you can pick from or lets you pick any perk.

| Class | 2nd | 4th | 6th | 8th | 10th |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Operator** | Crafting, exploration, or intrigue | Any | Crafting, exploration, or intrigue | Any | Crafting, exploration, or intrigue |
| **Scout** | Exploration, interpersonal, or intrigue | Any | Any | Any | Any |

Other classes list their perk grants in their own chapters. “Any” means any of the six types, lore and supernatural included.

## Test results in perk text

Perks use Ghostwire’s result wording: **low**, **middle**, and **high** result, the same bands as a power roll. “Improve the outcome by one result” means low becomes middle, or middle becomes high.

## Foundry notes

- The **Ghostwire Perks** compendium has one folder per perk type, plus a **Perk Abilities** folder for the three abilities perks grant (Veil Trick, Ghost Touch, Wire Whisper).
- When a class grants a perk, the level-up window lists the Ghostwire perks that match the allowed types. Draw Steel’s own perks are hidden and can’t be dropped in.

# 0.3.141 — Foundry checklist

The **Chase HUD** (vehicle combat HUD). It runs the 0.3.138 chase round checklist in one applet: roster, crew stations, range-band track, its own phase stepper, Integrity write-through, sound with a HUD-only mute, Director band fiat, and a Director review of every roll before it counts.

Worlds on 0.3.140: module update + refresh (`F5`). Only the `rulebook` pack was rebuilt (the Machines › Vehicles page gained a *Chase HUD (Foundry)* section), so **reload the world after pull**. Nothing needs Recall + Deploy.

**Prep.** One scene, one GM client and one player client if you can (a second browser works). Deploy **two** machines from hero inventories — e.g. a **Bulldog** (Handling 1) and a **Scrap-Bike** (Handling 4) — and have at least three hero / NPC tokens on the scene. Give one machine a **Sensor Pod** and a weaponry kit with a mounted gun if you want the edge and a damage suggestion to show. Have one hero **Mount** the Bulldog (0.3.137 passengers).

---

## 1. Open the HUD and start a chase

1. Token controls (left toolbar) → **Chase HUD** (crashing-car icon).
   → The window opens on the scene you are viewing and says no chase is running. The GM sees **Start a chase**; a player sees *The Director starts one*.
2. GM: **Start a chase**. → Round 1, phase **0 · Setup**, the eight-step phase strip (0 → 1 → 2a → 2b → 2c → 3 → 4 → 5) and an empty band track.
3. Open the Combat tracker. → No encounter was created. The HUD never touches Foundry Combat.

## 2. Two vehicles and crew stations

1. GM: pick the Bulldog in **Add vehicle** → **Add vehicle**; then select the Scrap-Bike token on the canvas → **Add selected tokens**.
   → Two cards. The first is **Crew** and the **Lead**; the second is **Opposition** at **Medium** on the track. The hero who mounted the Bulldog is already in its **Ports**.
2. Each card shows **Integrity** (current / max with a bar), **Handling** (1 vs 4), **Speed** (+ speed band), **Scale**, the band, and any chase mods as tags (Sensor Pod, Signal Mule, …).
3. Seat a hero as **Pilot** and one as **Systems** on each vehicle, and one or two as **Turrets**. Seating a second Pilot swaps the seat; Turrets take several. Leave one station empty → it reads *empty — skipped*.
4. The player client sees the same cards read-only (no selects, no Remove), and the band on the track.

## 3. Run one phase each (phase by phase, all vehicles together)

1. GM: **Next phase** → **1 · Pilots**. The Scrap-Bike card shows *Pilot contest against Bulldog* with a *wants to* Close / Open / Hold / Ram pick for each side; the lead card says it is contested by every other Pilot.
2. **Roll**. → One chat card with both pilots' dice. The Scrap-Bike (Handling 4 vs 1) shows *higher Handling 4 vs 1: edge*; an untrained pilot shows *untrained: bane*. You hear the pilot sound. A **Director review** window opens (see 7).
3. **Next** → **2a Sensor lock**: Systems → pick a target → **Sensor lock**. Sensor Pod shows *edge*; a Ghost Coat target shows *bane*. Confirm → the card tags *Lock on …*.
4. **Next** → **2b Jam / spoof**: the other vehicle's Systems jams. Two sets of dice (jammer vs lock-holder). Confirm a landed jam → the target loses its lock tag and shows *Jammed this round*. Nothing about Uptime changes.
5. **Next** → **2c Terrain read**: roll, pick *Edge to* Pilot or Turrets; confirm → *Terrain edge* tag.
6. **Next** → **3 Gunners**: each Turret row has Target + Weapon; **Fire**. With a lock: *bane*; without: *double bane*. A passenger in Ports gets **Fire (passenger)** with *speed bane*. Confirm a hit → the target shows *N damage queued*.
7. Use **Pass** on one station → it shows **Done** without rolling.
8. **Next** → **4 Integrity** → **5 End** → **Next** → **Round 2 · 1 Pilots**. Jams and the Gunner edge are gone; bands and live locks are still there.

## 4. Integrity write-through

1. In **4 · Integrity**, GM: **Apply queued damage**. Change one amount in the dialog, then **Apply to the vehicles**.
   → A chat card: *X takes N Integrity damage — now V / M*. The card's Integrity bar drops, and you hear the damage clank.
2. Open that vehicle's **Actor sheet**. → Its Integrity is the new number. The token bar agrees. Below 50% / 25% the On Fire / Crippled status lands on its own.
3. GM: **Integrity** button on a card → *Repair* 5 → sheet and HUD both go up by 5.
4. Damage a vehicle to 0. → *wrecked* chat line, the wreck sound, the card greys out with **Wrecked**, it drops out of contests, and *The chase is over* shows when only one vehicle is left.

## 5. Sound and the HUD mute

1. Every roll and commit so far made a sound: pilot contest, sensor lock, jam, terrain read, gunnery, passenger fire, Integrity damage, repair, wreck, band shift.
2. On the **player** client click the speaker button (**Sound on** → **Muted**). Roll something from the GM client.
   → The GM hears it; the muted player does not. Foundry's own volume sliders did not move. Other Ghostwire sounds (ability SFX, crit sting) still play for the player.
3. The same toggle is **Configure Settings → Mute Chase HUD sounds** (per client). World **SFX: enabled** off still silences the HUD for everyone.

## 6. Director band override

1. GM: on a non-lead card, change the **Band** select to **Ramming / Boarding**. → The track chip jumps at once and the band-shift sound plays. No roll, no review.
2. Use ◀ / ▶ to step it one band. Set it to **Broken off** → the vehicle is marked *Broken off* and leaves the contests. Set it back to **Close** → it is back in.
3. After a Pilot roll suggests *Medium*, set **Band after this** to **Extreme** in the review and confirm → the track shows Extreme (fiat beats the roll).
4. **Make lead** on the other vehicle → the crown moves; every band now reads as distance to the new lead.

## 7. Director outcome adjust before commit

1. GM rolls anything. The review window opens: *Nothing changes until you confirm.* Per roll it offers **Tier**, **Winner** / **Band after this** / ram damage (Pilots), **Lock acquired** (2a), **Jam lands** (2b), **Success** + **Edge to** (2c), **Hit** + **Integrity damage** (3). Changing the tier on a gunnery roll re-fills the damage from the weapon's tier numbers.
2. **Discard** → nothing changes, and the station is still available to roll.
3. Roll again → **Keep for later** → the HUD shows *Waiting for the Director (1)* with **Review** / trash. **Review** reopens the same window.
4. **Player** client: roll for a crew member that player owns. → The dice post to chat; the GM gets *X rolled in the Chase HUD … Review it there*, and the pending row appears. Nothing on the cards changes until the GM confirms. A player cannot roll for crew they do not own.

## 8. Journal and VOIDMARK

1. **Ghostwire Rulebook → Machines → Vehicles**: right after the *Chase round checklist* is **Chase HUD (Foundry)** — how to open it, seats (Ports = passengers), the track, the phases, the Director's review, band fiat, Integrity write-through and the mute.
2. Ask VOIDMARK, with **no Clear Thread**: *"chase HUD"*, then *"vehicle combat HUD"*, then *"How do I open the Chase HUD?"* → each retrieves the new section.
3. **End chase** (GM) → confirm → the HUD is back to *No chase is running*. Every vehicle's Integrity stays what the chase left it at.

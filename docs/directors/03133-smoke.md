# 0.3.133 — Foundry smoke checklist

**What this wave changed:** thirty-one summon rows got real art and a one-time world pass to bring
existing copies along; pact spirits arrive tinted from a pact read four different ways; **Take
Cover** stopped doing nothing; and three SFX rules stopped matching the wrong string. Two new
scripts (`scripts/take-cover.mjs`, `scripts/summon-art.mjs`), edits to `veil-summons`,
`cover-conceal`, `conceal`, `ritual-seal` and `sfx`, and **pack JSON did change** — the summons,
abilities, gear and pregens packs were rebuilt with Foundry closed.

**The five things to prove:** a Compile Agent walks in wearing its own face; a Light priest's spirit
is warm-gold and a Dark priest's is violet; Take Cover applies cover and *moving* takes it away
while a Scout's does not; Breach and Clear goes *bang* and Breathless Hit does not scream; a sealed
Calling Working actually puts something on the map.

Node smokes first, all green before you open Foundry:

```
node tools/wave-03133-smoke.mjs
node tools/wave-03132-smoke.mjs
node tools/cover-conceal-smoke.mjs
node tools/ritual-seal-smoke.mjs
node tools/hacker-agents-smoke.mjs
node tools/sp-el-foundry-wave-03123-smoke.mjs
node tools/scout-sp-wave-03124-smoke.mjs
node tools/g3-lang-style-tokens-smoke.mjs
node tools/pregen-regen-smoke.mjs
```

## 0. Set the stage

1. Open a **gridded** scene with room to walk five or six squares.
2. Have to hand: a **Technomancer** (sprites + Compile Agent), a **Street Priest** with the **Light**
   pact and a second with the **Dark** pact (Vessa is the shipped Light one), an **Elementalist**,
   an **Operator** with a loaded sidearm, and a **Scout**.
3. Put one enemy token **adjacent** to the hero and one **well out of reach**, so you can roll a
   melee and a ranged attack against the same target.
4. First boot on this version: watch the console. You should see, once,
   `draw-steel-ghostwire | summon art sync 0.3.133: N document(s) repointed` (or nothing, if the
   world has no old summons) and, if any hero had the old card,
   `draw-steel-ghostwire | Take Cover migration 0.3.133: N embedded copy/copies cleaned`.
   **Reload a second time — neither line should appear again.** That is the whole point of the
   version gate.

## 1. PRIME — summon art, everywhere it lives

### 1a. The compendium

1. Open **Ghostwire Summons & Machines**.
2. **Agents** folder: all twelve rows show art, and all three **Probe** tiers show the *same* Probe
   art (likewise Spike, Daemon, Watchdog). No coloured discs.
3. **Sprites** folder: all three **Ward** tiers share one ward image; same for Data, Attack, Machine.
   No stock Foundry icons (no blue lightning bolt, no green cross, no teal eye).
4. **Elementals**: Ember / Zephyr / Boulder are Michael's new plates; Ranks 1–3 are the three bound
   elementals; **Greater** is the Living Mountain, not a fireball icon.
5. **Pact Spirits**: Guardian / Warrior / Hunter are the grayscale tint-ready bases. They should look
   **grey**, not tinted — the colour is added when one is summoned.

### 1b. On the canvas

6. Drag an **Agent — Watchdog (Minor)** onto the scene. The token wears the watchdog art.
7. As the Technomancer, use **Compile Agent** and **Compile Sprite**. Each places a token with the
   new art, at its normal footprint.

### 1c. The world sync did not eat anything

8. Pick any summon Actor in the sidebar, set its portrait to something of your own
   (`Configure → Image`), reload, and confirm **your** art survived. The pass only replaces paths the
   module itself shipped.

## 2. Pact tint — Light is gold, Dark is violet

1. As the **Light** priest, use **Invoke the Pact** and take a middle or high Bind.
2. The spirit that lands is tinted **warm gold `#fff1b8`**. Open it: `Pact: Light` is **enabled**,
   `Pact: Dark` is **disabled**, and the sheet's flag reads `pact: light`.
3. Right-click the **placed token → Configure → Appearance**: `Tint` reads `#fff1b8`. (This is the
   half that used to be missed — the prototype was tinted and the token on the map was not.)
4. Repeat as the **Dark** priest: **violet `#c9a0ff`**, `Pact: Dark` enabled, `Pact: Light` disabled.
5. Take a priest and **delete their Light Pact / Dark Pact feature**, then summon again. The spirit
   arrives **grey** and you get the toast: *"…has sworn neither the Light nor the Dark pact…"*.
   Nothing errors, nothing guesses.
6. On that grey spirit, enable `Pact: Dark` from the sheet. It should tint violet immediately — the
   manual path (`syncPactTint`) still works and agrees on the hex.
7. **Sentinel Spirit** is deliberately *not* a token summon. Using it should still place nothing.

## 3. PRIME — Take Cover

1. As any hero, use **Take Cover** from the Abilities tab.
2. **Cover/Conceal** appears on the token (shield icon), and you get the line *"…takes cover: ranged
   attacks against them take a bane until they move."*
3. Read the card: the description now says Cover/Conceal, one bane on **ranged**, melee unaffected,
   ends when you move. **There is no `[[/apply]]` link and no "double bane" anywhere.** Open the
   ability's Effects tab — it should be **empty**.
4. Shoot that hero from the **far** enemy: the roll dialog shows **1 bane** for the target.
5. Hit that hero from the **adjacent** enemy: **no bane**. Melee is untouched.
6. **Move the hero one square.** Cover/Conceal drops, and you get *"…broke cover."*
7. Nudge a hero *inside* the same square (small drag, no cell change) with cover up: the cover
   **stays**. Squares, not pixels.
8. **The collision test.** Take Cover with the **Scout**, then roll **Conceal** and take a middle or
   high result (so Conceal also grants Cover/Conceal). Now **move**. Cover/Conceal must **stay** —
   only Take Cover's claim on it went away. Move again: it still stays, because Conceal's cover does
   not end on a move.
9. **The Director test.** Toggle Cover/Conceal onto an NPC by hand from the token HUD, then move that
   NPC. The status **stays** — nothing the module put there, nothing the module takes away.
10. Any **pregen** you open should show the same clean card (no retired effect) without you doing
    anything, because the migration already ran.

## 4. PRIME — Breach and Clear, and Breathless

1. As the **Operator**, use **Breach and Clear** with a loaded sidearm.
   * It **sounds like a pistol** (`pistol-single.ogg`), not like a door being scanned.
   * It spends **1 round** off the magazine.
   * With **0 rounds** loaded it **refuses before the roll** and says so.
   * With **two** loaded guns it asks **which gun** first, then fires the one you picked.
2. As the **Scout**, use **Breathless Hit**. It plays the melee weapon impact (`sword-crit.ogg`).
   **It must not scream.**
3. Spot-check that nothing else moved: **Quarry** still plays the mark sting, **Hex Round** still
   plays its own dart sound, **Bolt Barrage** is still a spell, **Lay on Hands** is still the strong
   holy heal, **Invoke the Pact** still plays the summon sound.
4. If any card sounds wrong, open its sheet: the **Ghostwire SFX** block names the rule it matched,
   which is the fastest way to say *which* rule to argue with.

## 5. F8 — a Calling Working calls something

1. Give a leader a **Calling** Ritual Formula — **Watcher** is the cheapest (Magnitude 2, General).
2. Run the Working and **Seal** it with a middle or high roll.
3. A summon token is **placed on the scene** beside the leader (Watcher → the Minor Watchdog agent),
   and the chat card says a summon was made rather than *"the Director places what answered"*.
4. Open the summoned Actor: it carries the **Ritual Effect** block under
   `flags.draw-steel-ghostwire.ritualEffect`, naming the Working.
5. A **tier 1 (hung)** seal still leaves **nothing**.
6. Spot-check one more: **Invoke Greater Shape** (Elementalist) should call the **Greater Living
   Mountain**; **Chassis Dressing** (Technomancer) should call a **Machine Sprite**.
7. A **Ward** Formula (e.g. Ward the Room) still places the **scene marker**, and the marker now
   wears `sprite-ward.webp`. It must not summon anything.

## 6. Regression sweep

* Open every pregen sheet: no console errors, portraits and round tokens unchanged.
* Roll a Scout **Conceal** high: Invisible still lands, and **moving still drops Invisible only**.
* **Dismiss Elemental** / **Dismiss Spirit** / **Decompile Sprite** / **Decompile Agent** still put
  their summons away, and the tokens leave with them.
* Flanking, Spot Target, hit FX and the gun picker (0.3.132) are untouched — a quick shot and a quick
  melee swing should behave exactly as they did last wave.

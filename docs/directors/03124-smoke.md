## B2 — Conceal, the free Scout maneuver

1. Open **Wren Sable-Corvin** from *Ghostwire Pregens*. **Conceal** is on the sheet, at **0 Advantage**,
   as a **maneuver**. It is a class feature, not a Hunter one — make a Ghost or a Face and it is there too.
2. Drop Wren on a scene with one enemy token and use **Conceal**.

| Rolled | The card should say | The token should get |
|---|---|---|
| **low (≤11)** | *Nothing happens. You are still standing right there.* | nothing |
| **middle (12–16)** | *You gain Cover/Conceal.* | **Cover/Conceal** only |
| **high (17+)** | *You gain Cover/Conceal, and you are Invisible until you move.* | **Cover/Conceal** *and* **Invisible** |

3. On a **high**: the token should actually **disappear** for a player who does not own it. Not dimmed —
   gone. Log in as a player with a second hero and confirm. A natural 20 counts as high.
4. With Invisible up, target Wren with a ranged strike from a token that has **Cyber-Eyes** or better
   (any see-invisible optic): she is visible again to that token inside its band, and the attack still
   takes the Cover/Conceal **bane**.
5. **Move Wren one square.** The **Invisible drops** and the **Cover/Conceal stays**. Check it three ways:
   drag the token, use a forced move / push against her, and teleport her. All three drop the Invisible.
6. Nudge the token *inside its own square* (a short drag that lands in the same cell). The Invisible
   should **survive** — only a square change ends it.
7. Settings → Ghostwire → **“Conceal applies its result automatically.”** Turn it off, roll Conceal
   again: the card still prints the tier, and no status is applied. Turn it back on.

## B1 — Hard Tag costs 1 Advantage

8. On Wren, **Hard Tag** now shows a cost of **1 Advantage**.
9. Out of combat with 0 Advantage banked, using it is still allowed (Draw Steel's out-of-combat rule).
   **In combat with 0 Advantage**, using it is refused with a toast naming the cost.
10. In combat with Advantage in hand: using it spends **1**, Marks the target, and the Marked effect icon
    lands on the enemy. The **first strike per round** bonus is unchanged.
11. Spending **extra** Advantage on top still buys **1 surge per extra point** — the surge rider is now
    priced *beyond* the first Advantage, and the card text says so.

    Sacrificial Offer comes **off the sheet** and nothing is added in its place — an optional pick is
    the player's to make again.

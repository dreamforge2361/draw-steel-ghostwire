# 0.3.137 — Foundry checklist

Two things to check in a live world: **riders stay aboard**, and **four buttons left the Token toolbar
without taking their features with them**.

Scripts and smoke only this wave — no art, no pack rebuild, no dsid changed. A world on 0.3.136 needs
nothing but the module update and a refresh (`F5`).

---

## 1. Mount / Dismount — riders stay on the hull

Use a **vehicle** with room for a crew (a car or a heavy — a Micro drone carries nobody by design) and
two heroes. The Bulldog is the one Michael screenshotted the bug on.

1. **Remount a hero.** Select one or two heroes, target the vehicle, press **Mount** on the Token
   toolbar (or the `fa-users-line` button on the vehicle's Token HUD).
   → The notification names who came aboard and the seats used, e.g. `2 / 4`.
2. **Confirm the rider looks on-hull.** The hero should be drawn at **half scale**, sitting **on the
   painted chassis** — on its rim, not floating a square or two off its flank in open road. Check this
   on a **long** vehicle especially (a 1×4 flatbed or a 2×4 heavy): that aspect ratio is what was
   broken. With one rider aboard, seat 0 is the **nose**.
3. **Drag the vehicle.** Pick it up and drag it several squares, including through a door or gate.
   → The riders come with it and land back on the hull. They must **not** be left behind in open space
   still wearing the mounted look, and they must **not** be stopped short against the wall they were
   carried through.
4. **Rotate the vehicle.** Turn it 90°, then 180° (Token HUD rotation, or the config sheet).
   → The crew **swings with the hull** and keeps its relative seats — the hero in the nose seat is
   still in the nose.
5. Also worth a pass: **nudge it with the arrow keys** (keyboard movement is a different Foundry
   movement path from a drag), and **change its elevation** — the crew should track both.
6. **Dismount.** Press **Dismount** with the rider selected, or the `fa-person-through-window` button
   on the vehicle's HUD to empty it out.
   → Each rider is back at **its own art scale and elevation**, standing in its **own clear square**
   grid-snapped beside the hull — not stacked on the driver's door.
7. **Free the crew the hard way.** With riders aboard, **Recall** or delete the vehicle token.
   → Everybody is released automatically, restored to their own scale, and the chat/notification says
   how many.
8. **A stray drag must not unseat anybody.** Try to drag a *mounted rider*.
   → Refused with a warning that tells you to use **Dismount**. The rider does not move and does not
   quietly step off.

Weapon hardpoints (`Mount` / `Unmount` on a Turret Ring) are a **different feature** and were not
touched this wave — if they still work, that is the expected result, not a coincidence.

## 2. Toolbar declutter — four buttons gone, four features still there

The Token toolbar had grown past what fits down the left edge of the canvas. These four buttons were
removed in 0.3.137. **Directors use the macros pack or the keybinds for Pay / Spend / Taint / Black
Market sell** — plus the Token HUD, which kept all four.

1. Open the **Token** toolbar (the left edge of the canvas) as the **Director**.
   → **Gone:** Black Market: sell, Director: Pay Hero, Director: Spend Hero, Director: Taint +1.
   → **Still there:** VOIDMARK, Wired Console, Wired Minimap, Ritual Working, Run Generator,
   Place Kiosk, Place Locker, **Mount**, **Dismount**.
2. Open the **Macros** compendium (Ghostwire Macros) and confirm all four still ship and still run:
   **Black Market Sell**, **Director Pay Hero**, **Director Spend Hero**, **Director Taint +1**.
   Drag them to your hotbar — that is the intended home for them now.
3. Check the **keybindings** (Configure Controls → Ghostwire) still fire: Black Market sell (open to
   players), Director wealth (Director-only), Director Taint +1 (Director-only).
4. Right-click a hero token and check the **Token HUD** still carries all four doors: the sack
   (Black Market sell), the yen sign (Director wealth), the biohazard (Taint +1) — and the ride
   buttons from item 1.
5. Right-click an **Item on a hero sheet** → **Sell** still opens the Black Market. That entry was
   never on the toolbar and is unchanged.

Nothing was removed from the rules, the purse or the Taint track — only the toolbar buttons.

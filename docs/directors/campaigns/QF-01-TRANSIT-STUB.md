## Scene 2 — Party-token travel (Switchboard → Slackwater)

**When:** After A1 Mama’s brief; before A2 Slackwater arrival (A2 becomes “after disembark”).

**Deadhead cross-use:** Deadhead Scene **1.15** reuses these same licensed Flats transit read-alouds and texture for the short hop **Mama’s alley → Rack & Rest** (default Grey Cab; Veinline option). **QF-01 Scene 2 remains unchanged: Switchboard → Slackwater.**

**Table procedure:** Offer the crew a licensed Flats ride. Place **one** party vehicle token on a travel strip / abstract lane (or narrate only). Hand-wave districts between Switchboard and Slackwater — do not invent a full district crawl unless the table wants color.

### Table options (short read-alouds)

#### Option A — Grey Cab Services (“hail a Grey”)

> The hail-ping answers in Meridian chrome — a soft chime, a fare meter waking on Argent’s ledger. A closed Ferrum pod slides out of the cushioned river at limiter height, canopy sealed, no driver face, only a lane light. Inside: quiet seats, scrubbed air, the meter ticking polite. Outside: the Flats skim past under the next level’s belly. You are traffic, not a target, until someone decides otherwise.

**Director color:** Quieter, more private, costs more. Meter is ARG; chassis is FER; MER sold the ping. Good for crews carrying a sealed sleeve who do not want a car full of wage-bodies.

#### Option B — Veinline Civic (“ride the Vein”)

> The Vein arrives as a long gunmetal car hung under civic teal lamps — Ferrum rail bones, wet windows, a voice announcing stops in three tongues. You pack in with shift workers and wet coats. The tram hangs the culvert line and the hive’s underbelly rolls by in strobes of sodium and rain. Cheap. Public. Seen.

**Director color:** Cheaper, louder, more eyes. Civic branding. Good when the crew wants cover-as-crowd or is broke after Mama’s Low tease.

### Journey beats (hand-wave districts between)

1. **Hail / board** — Grey ping or Vein platform (Switchboard edge).
2. **River skim / rail hang** — 1–2 beats of street weather: neon smear, drone lanes above, black-water smell rising toward Slackwater.
3. **Disembark** — cut to A2 Slackwater canal approach (rain + Undertow chalk). Party token off; foot tokens on.

Optional speed bump (one only): Meridian ad-glitch on the meter; Ferrum maintenance drone welding a rail; Argent “insufficient float” flash if someone tries to spoof fare. Do not turn Scene 2 into a fight unless the table insists.

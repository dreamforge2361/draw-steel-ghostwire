## Campaign shape (sketch)

Use the Quiet Floor as a **climbing clock**, not a day-one city-killer:

1. **Seed** — telemetry, one wrong drone, a dead-air block that should not be quiet.
2. **Jobs** — Switchboard / Flats / Sinks work that brushes the Nine; award Victories when beats matter.
3. **Short stops** between sessions mid-job — Recoveries OK; do **not** bank XP or pay Lifestyle.
4. **Full respite** when the job ends — bank XP, maybe level, payday, Lifestyle, Projects.
5. **Escalate** the Floor only when the fiction has earned a larger quiet.

Named fixtures that punish lingering are clocks (`27`). Do not open with city-killing Dark bot swarms.

## Session budget (default cadence)

~**2.5 Victories / session**. Levels **1→5** at **5 XP** (~2 sessions/level); **5→10** at **8 XP** (~3 sessions/level). Dial 6/9 or 4/6 in the pacing doc if the table wants slower/faster.

# 0.3.142 + 0.3.143 — one-pass Foundry smoke

Two waves, one sitting. 0.3.142 wrote **Biofeedback** down once and gave the Director a macro to run
it by hand. 0.3.143 taught the **node applet** to notice the four ICE triggers on its own — and to
**ask before it bites, every time**. They are the same feature in two halves, so smoke them together.

The full per-wave pages are still there if a step goes wrong and you want the long version:
[`03142-smoke.md`](03142-smoke.md) · [`03143-smoke.md`](03143-smoke.md).

**Offline first.** Run these before you open Foundry; if either is red, stop:

```
node tools/wave-03142-smoke.mjs
node tools/wave-03143-smoke.mjs
```

**Setup (once, for both halves).** A world on the stock `draw-steel` system with
**Draw Steel – Ghostwire Build 0.3.143** enabled, logged in as **Director / GM**.

- One hero you are happy to damage, carrying a **Street Deck** (Biofeedback Resistance **2**). A
  Hacker pregen such as **Vira** is ideal. Note their Stamina before you start.
- A scene with their token on it, and a **Wire Kit** or deck on the sheet so **Connect** works.
- Open the **Wired Console** (network icon, Token controls) and build a small board: a **Track 2,
  Rating 3** host, a **Track 2, Rating 4** host, and one **Track 1** object (a maglock or a light).
  **Reveal** all three and **Place on canvas**.

**The one thing to watch all the way through.** Neither wave is allowed to move Stamina without
showing you a number first. The macro asks. The applet asks. If Stamina ever moves with no dialog,
that is the bug, and nothing else on this page matters until it is explained.

# Part A — 0.3.142: the procedure, the macro, the floor

## 1. The macro exists, and is Director-only

1. **Compendiums → Ghostwire Macros**. **Director: Apply Biofeedback** is there, beside *Director:
   Pay Hero* and *Director: Spend Hero*, on a blue lightning-bolt icon.
2. There is **no new Token toolbar button** — the 0.3.137 declutter holds. The tool is the macro plus
   an unbound keybinding under **Configure Controls**.
3. Log in as a **player** and run it from a hotbar slot: *"Only the Director can apply Biofeedback"*,
   and **no Stamina moves**.

**Pass:** present, Director-only.

## 2. It reads the live state and the carried deck

1. As Director, target the hero while **Disconnected** and run the macro. The target line reads
   *`<hero>` — Disconnected — no Biofeedback, resistance N*, and **Connection state** says
   **Auto — Disconnected**.
2. Cancel. Step them to **Linked**, then **Overlay**, then **Jacked In** (node applet **Connect** /
   **Toggle**, or the **Cycle Wire State** macro), reopening each time. The **Auto —** label follows.
3. With the Street Deck on the sheet, **Biofeedback Resistance** shows **Auto — 2** at Echelon 1.
   Strip the deck and it shows **Auto — 0**.

**Pass:** no typing needed to get the right two numbers.

## 3. The four steps, and the two locked numbers

1. Hero **Overlay**. Run the macro at **Node Rating 3**, state **Auto**, resistance **Auto**, trigger
   *Low (≤11) Wired Power Roll vs active ICE (R3+)*. Apply.
2. Chat prints, in order: `Base: Rating 3 host, Biofeedback Value 8` · `Overlay — ×0.5, round down,
   min 1 → 4` · `Less Biofeedback Resistance 2 → 2 damage` · `Stamina <before> → <after>`.
3. Stamina on the sheet and the token bar dropped by exactly **2**.
4. Hero **Jacked In**, same Rating 3. The card reads `→ 12`, then `→ 10 damage`, and Stamina drops by
   **10**.

**Pass:** 8 → 4 → 2 Overlaid; 8 → 12 → 10 Jacked In. Remember these two numbers — Part B produces
them again from the applet.

## 4. Linked and Disconnected take nothing

1. Hero **Linked**. Run the macro at **Rating 5** (base 22), resistance **Auto**. The card reads
   `Linked — no Biofeedback → 0` and `No Biofeedback lands…`. **Stamina does not move.**
2. Repeat **Disconnected**. Same answer.

**Pass:** a Rating 5 host cannot touch a Linked or Disconnected runner.

## 5. The Winded floor

1. Hero **Jacked In**, Stamina edited down to **3**. Run the macro at **Rating 3** (10 damage after
   resistance 2).
2. Stamina lands on **1**, not −7. The card adds *"Winded floor: Biofeedback stopped at 1 Stamina.
   `<hero>` is Winded, not Dying — but a bullet still kills."*
3. Run it **again**, same settings. Stamina **stays at 1** — the floor never heals.
4. Now damage them by ordinary means (a strike, or editing Stamina to 0). They go to **0 and Dying**:
   the floor covers Biofeedback only.

**Pass:** held at 1, Winded not Dying, idempotent, and bullets still kill.

## 6. North Substation, and the rules that agree with the macro

1. **Ghostwire Rulebook → Ghostwire Systems → The Wired System**. Find **Biofeedback -- the full
   procedure** (four steps, the worked line `8 × 0.5 = 4 → minus 2 → 2 Stamina damage`, and **The
   Winded floor (both sources)**), and under it **ICE attack triggers -- when ICE actually bites**,
   which opens *"ICE does not get a free attack every round."* and lists **four**.
2. In the **Trace Alert** table, the **9–11** row delivers the **hunt bite**, not a bane.
3. Same journal, page **North Substation — a worked Wired run**: Act 0 through Act 6 plus a
   Technomancer appendix. **Act 2's bite is 2. Act 4's is 10** — the two numbers from check 3.
4. **The Technomancer → Biofeedback — The Cost of Overreach**: the same Winded floor, worded the same
   way, with the 5+ Resonance Physique test unchanged.
5. **The Hacker → Cyberdecks (Your Kit)**: Biofeedback Resistance is per-Echelon, applied after the
   connection scale, with the five deck numbers printed (Scrapdeck 1 · Street Deck 2 · Ghostbox 2 ·
   Blackdeck 3 · Fairlight Ghost 3).
6. Director cue sheet on disk: `docs/directors/north-substation-masterwork.md`.

**Pass:** no page contradicts the macro, and no second procedure exists anywhere.

# Part B — 0.3.143: the applet notices, and asks

Everything below happens on the **node applet** (double-click a node token, or the network button on
its Token HUD). Part A's two numbers should come back out of it unchanged.

## 7. The reactive low bite

1. Open the **Rating 3** node. Select your hero. **Connect** (→ Linked), **Toggle** once (→ Overlay).
2. Fire **Read/Write** (or Search, or Navigate) until a Power Roll comes in at **11 or under**.
3. A card opens: **ICE bite — `<hero>`**, headed *"ICE reactive bite on `<hero>`?"*, with a hint line
   naming the node and the roll, and the same four lines as the macro:
   `Base: Rating 3 host, Biofeedback Value 8` · `Overlay → 4` · `Less Biofeedback Resistance 2 → 2
   damage` · `Stamina <before> → <after>`.
4. **Cancel.** Stamina does not move, and **nothing posts to chat.**
5. Roll low again and press **Apply 2**. Stamina drops by **2** and the ordinary 0.3.142 Biofeedback
   card posts, trigger named. **That is check 3's number, produced by the applet.**
6. **Toggle** to **Jacked In** and repeat: the same host now offers **10**.

**Pass:** the applet and the macro produce the same two numbers, and Cancel is free.

## 8. The three silences

Each of these should produce **no card at all**:

1. Hero back to **Linked** — roll as low as you like against the Rating 3 host.
2. A **Rating 2** node from Overlay — passive ICE is flavour.
3. The **Track 1** object from Overlay — Track 1 carries no ICE.

**Pass:** the applet asks only when the rules say it may.

## 9. The failed breach at Rating 4+

1. Open the **Rating 4** node (Breach **DC 17**). From Overlay or Jacked In, fire **Read/Write** and
   land **12–16** — a middle result that still misses the DC.
2. One card: *"Failed breach — ICE bites `<hero>`?"*, hint naming the Rating, the DC and the roll.
   The base is the Rating 4 line, **13**.
3. Land **11 or under** on the same host: **two cards**, one after the other. Two different triggers
   on one roll are two bites, and you may Apply or Cancel each independently.
4. Fire **Scan** and roll low: **no breach card** — Scan is observation, not a breach.

**Pass:** DC-aware, and "two triggers, two bites" is real.

## 10. The Alert strip, 5 / 9 / 12

1. On the node applet, the twelve Trace Alert steps now outline **5**, **9** and **12**, with a
   three-line legend under the bar (Malice · Hunt · Lockout). Reached steps read brighter.
2. In the Console, raise that node's Alert past **5** → chat announces the Malice step. Past **9** →
   announces the hunt. **Max (12)** → announces lockout and counter-trace.
3. **Reset** (down to 6): **nothing is announced.** Coming down is bookkeeping.
4. Jump from **4 to 9** in one move: **both** announcements post, 5 then 9.

**Pass:** one announcement per upward crossing, none downward.

## 11. The hunt bite at end of turn

1. Set the **Rating 3** node to Trace Alert **9**. The applet shows a red hunting notice.
2. Hero **Overlay** (or Jacked In). Start a **combat** with them in it.
3. End their turn. A card opens: *"Hunt bite on `<hero>`?"* — same math, same Apply/Cancel.
4. **Cancel**, advance to their next turn end: it offers again, **once per turn**.
5. **Apply** until a bite would take them to 0: the card ends with the **Winded floor** line and they
   hold at **1**.
6. **Toggle to Linked** → end of turn produces **no card**. **Jack Out** → no card. Set Alert to
   **12** → no card (12 is lockout, not the hunt band).

**Pass:** one bite per ended turn at Alert 9–11 while immersed, and nothing else.

## 12. The Malice ICE surge

1. On the Rating 3 node (as Director), press **Malice ICE surge** under the Alert strip. The tooltip
   names your current Malice.
2. Pick a target if asked — the compiler is preselected; only Overlay / Jacked In runners are listed.
3. **Cancel** the confirm card: nothing happens, no chat.
4. Press again and **Apply**: Stamina moves by the card's number, the Biofeedback card posts with the
   trigger named, and a short **Director: Malice ICE surge** card reminds you to adjust Malice.
5. **Your Malice count has not changed.** Ghostwire does not spend it for you — that is deliberate and
   the card says so.
6. The button is **not** on a Track 1 object, and **players do not see it**.

**Pass:** Director-only, confirmed, and your own Malice tracker is left alone.

## 13. Soft refuses — why, and what next

1. Hero **Linked**. Hover the greyed-out **Scan**: the tooltip gives the rule *and* the next step —
   *"…Scan requires Overlay or Jacked In — Toggle Connection State first."*
2. Fire a refused verb: a **Refused** card is whispered to you with two lines, **why** then **hint**.
   Nothing rolls and nothing is spent.
3. **Disconnected** points at **Connect**; **already on-net** points at **Toggle**; **no interface**
   tells you to put a deck or commlink on the sheet.
4. From **Overlay**, fire **Ping** at the **Track 2** host: it refuses, explains that Track 2 is where
   the ICE lives and Ping never defeats ICE, and points at **Read/Write** or a Program.
5. Fire **Ping** at the **Track 1** object: it works normally.

**Pass:** every refuse answers both questions, and Ping knows its Track.

## 14. The Wire run checklist

1. Below the Alert strip, click **Wire run checklist**. Eight steps open in North Substation order:
   Connect → Linked → Broadcast · Toggle to Overlay · Scan / Deep Scan · Track 1 vs Track 2 · Watch
   lows against ICE · Integrity · Alert bands 1–4 / 5–8 / 9–11 / 12 · Decompile / Jack Out.
2. Tick two, untick one. Open a different node: the ticks are **per node**; the open/closed state is
   not.
3. As a **player**, the checklist is there with their own ticks. Neither of you sees the other's.
4. With every box ticked, the verbs gate exactly as before. **The checklist blocks nothing.**

**Pass:** guidance only, per user.

## 15. VOIDMARK knows about both halves

1. Open **VOIDMARK** (ghost icon), **Director** mode.
2. *"How does biofeedback work?"* → the four steps, the Rating table, the connection scale
   (Linked 0 · Overlay ×0.5 min 1 · Jacked In ×1.5), the resistance subtraction, the Winded floor.
   No ×1 rung.
3. *"Does ICE attack every round?"* → **no**, followed by the four triggers.
4. *"What happens at North Substation?"* → the worked run.
5. *"Does the node applet apply biofeedback automatically?"* → **no** — it raises a confirm card with
   the math prefilled, and Cancel changes nothing.
6. *"Can my Special Agent pull a downed runner out of a corridor?"* → **no**, Agents and sprites are
   Wire-only, with the Wire version of what you wanted offered instead.
7. Switch to **Runner** mode and ask 2 and 6 again. Same rules, in voice, no chapter filenames.

**Pass:** six answers, no contradiction with the journal or the applet.

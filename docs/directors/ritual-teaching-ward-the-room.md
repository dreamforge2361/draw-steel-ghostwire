# Teaching example — Ward the Room (Magnitude 1)

**Purpose:** Show players how Ritual Workings work end-to-end.  
**Working:** Ward the Room · Family Ward · Magnitude 1 · General (Elementalist / Street Priest / Technomancer Wire-skin)  
**Formula Item:** ritual scroll (or datachip for the Wire-chalk skin)

---

## Global rules (every Working)

- Rituals never spend **Essence**, **Conviction**, or **Resonance**. Those pools are encounter casting only.
- Ritual costs are **¥**, reagents, downtime / Project Points, Veil Price / spirit-attention (fiction), and Malice ticks.
- Formula Items are **found once** (data steal, faction/org, mentor, stolen grimoire, thin-place vision) — **not** vendor stock. Reagents may still be shoppable.
- Study time scales with **Magnitude** (spell it out — do not use “Mag” in player-facing rules):

| Magnitude | Study time |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 day |
| 2 | 3 days |
| 3 | 1 week |
| 4 | 2 weeks |
| 5 | 1 month |

---

## What Ward the Room does

Marks **one room** with a **Veil barrier (Magnitude 1)** until the next dawn.

- Spirits and Magic-keyword / Veil remote peeks take a **bane** to enter or pierce.
- People, bullets, and ordinary sensors still pass — not a physical wall.
- **Does not** affect Wire-only sprites, Agents, ICE, or pure Overlay / Jacked-In Matrix verbs. Wire room protection is **Data Ward** (Wire-rite).
- Magnitude 1 = teaching floor of the Ward family (one room, overnight, ambient bane). Higher Magnitude wards scale space, duration, or hardness.

## Story walkthrough

### 0. The need

The crew crashes at a Flats coffin-cube after a loud job. Mama’s people say sleep light — something with chrome eyes has been pacing the hall. The Street Priest, **Rook**, wants the cube quiet until morning. He wants a **Working**, not another fight.

### 1. Find the Formula

Rook does not buy this at a stall. After the last job, Mama’s people pass him a water-stained **scroll** lifted from a Quiet Floor courier: *Ward the Room*. The Item text is the full card — Magnitude, components with ¥, stages, payoff, scope. Onto his sheet. One find; he keeps it.

**Rule:** No Formula Item (or verified copy), no sealing. Finding is a one-time adventure beat.

### 2. Learn it (study)

Rook spends **one day** with the scroll (Magnitude 1 study time). Logic or Instinct; Occult / Religion edge if he has it. Director marks the Formula **learned** for Rook. Until then the scroll is loot.

**Rule:** Own ≠ cast.

### 3. Components (party funds)

The Formula lists each reagent and a total:

| Component | ¥ |
|---|---|
| Blessed salt (threshold line) | 25 |
| Resin incense | 50 |
| **Components total (Veil path)** | **75** |

Wire-skin alternate: circuit-chalk only — **Components total ¥40**.  
Optional thin-place ash: **¥ —** (fetch / mission; not in the total).

Rook’s crew subtracts **¥75** from the shared run fund at the botanica.

**Rule:** Formula card prints each ¥ line + one **Components total (¥)** to decrement from party funds.

### 4. Sanctum

The **room itself** is the temporary sanctum (¥0 materials). Rook clears the cube floor, marks the threshold, tells the crew not to scuff the line. Sanctum rating must be ≥ Magnitude; Magnitude 1 is trivial — the cube counts.

### 5. Sealing (the evening)

After dark the crew sits quiet. Rook opens the scroll, lays salt at the door and vents, lights the resin. A non-caster runner steadies the line and grants an edge. Rook makes one collaborative Power Roll (Persona as Street Priest; Logic for Elementalist; Logic / Matrix Theory for Wire-skin Technomancer).

He spends **downtime and reagents**. Price is negligible; if they sealed in a thin place, spirit-attention would tick +1.

The air goes a half-degree colder. The incense smell sticks. Dawn is the timer.

**Rule:** Sealing is Project / downtime, not an encounter ability.

### 6. Payoff

Until dawn, the cube is warded. A curious spirit or Magic remote peek takes a **bane** to push in. If a meat fight starts in the hall, the ward does nothing to bullets — but scry or ghost through the door and you feel the salt.

**Combat:** Ambient bane on Veil verbs into/through the room. No summon token. No resource drip to keep it up.

### 7. What breaks it

Someone kicks the salt line. Or an Unmaking Magnitude 1 Working. Or dawn.

## Player cheat-sheet (any Working)

1. **Find** Formula Item once (esoterica — not shops)  
2. **Study** it for the Magnitude study time → **learned** flag  
3. Buy/fetch **components** → subtract **Components total (¥)** from party funds  
4. Claim **sanctum** ≥ Magnitude  
5. **Seal** (downtime / climax) — ¥ already paid + Price  
6. Take **payoff**; watch duration and counters  

---

## Formula card checklist (Foundry Item)

The Item description holds the full Working: payoff, duration, impact, Magnitude, who may lead, study time, stage outline, component table with ¥ + **Components total**, sanctum notes, sealing notes, scope (Veil vs Wire), combat translation, counter condition.

# 0.3.134 — Foundry smoke checklist

Ritual text · firearm maker rename · arms-maker lore · element summons/FX/SFX · VOIDMARK summon fixes ·
the eight summon rules · Medic-only Field Triage · Rally to Me · Lay on Hands.

**Before you start**

1. Close Foundry, pull `gw-0.3.134`, reopen. The module version in **Manage Modules** should read **0.3.134**.
2. Open the console (F12) on load and look for these lines. They are the migrations; each one runs **once**:
   - `draw-steel-ghostwire | weapon rename 0.3.134: N document(s) renamed` — only if you had renamed guns in the world.
   - `draw-steel-ghostwire | Field Triage 0.3.134: removed from N non-Medic hero(es)`.
   - `draw-steel-ghostwire | ritual plain text: 46 Working(s) loaded`.
3. Reload once more. Both migration lines should now be **silent** — that is what "idempotent" means here.

Everything below is in brief order. `✅` when it matches, note what you saw when it does not.

## 1. Ritual text — plain story and plain rules on every Working

1. Open **Compendium → Ghostwire Gear → Ritual Formulas** and open **Formula: Ward the Room**.
2. Under the `Formula Item form:` line you should now see, in this order:
   - **The story:** a paragraph about walking the room with salt and resin;
   - **In plain words:** "Ghosts, spirits and any magic aimed into the room from outside get a bane — one penalty die on the roll. It does not stop people, bullets, cameras or hackers at all.";
   - **What this card's words mean here:** four bullets — **Magnitude**, **Scope**, **Seal**, **Expires** — each one about *this* ritual, not about rituals in general.
3. Open three more at random (try **Seal the Flat**, **True-Name Collar (Deep Bind)** and **Scrub the Ghost-Code**). Every one of the 46 has the same four bullets, with different content.
4. **The sealed card.** Run a Ward working to a middle or high seal (Ritual Working applet → Seal). The **Ritual Effect** marker it stands on the scene now carries the same six lines in its biography, under the Working / Family / Magnitude block.
5. Open **Compendium → Ghostwire Rulebook → The Veil** and find **Ritual Workings — General → Ward the Room**. Same block, in the chapter.

**What "good" looks like:** you can hand the Ward the Room card to somebody who has never played and they can tell you what it does, how long it lasts, and what happens on a bad roll.

3. Switch attunement to **cold** and fire again: icy pale blue/white. **acid**: green. **corruption**: violet. **poison**: sickly green with a purple core. **sonic**: pale cyan. **lightning**: blue-white.
4. Do the same for **Bolt Barrage**, **Elemental Wall**, **Void Vortex**, **World-Fissure** and **Cataclysm** — every attuned card follows the element.
5. Use **Elemental Shaping** in *damage* mode: the beat is tinted to your attunement too.
6. With a second client connected, confirm the **other player sees the same colour** (the relay carries the element).

## 8. The eight summon rules

1. Read **How to command summons** in each of: **Elementalist**, **Street Priest**, **The Veil**, **Constructs, Pets & Drones FAQ**, **Summon Stat Blocks**. All five say the same eight things.
2. Summon a **Bound Elemental (Rank 2)**. It now **has abilities** — it used to have none at all:
   - **Elemental Lash** at **6 / 9 / 12 + Logic**, typed to the element you summoned it with;
   - a **Command** card printing the 2d10 + Logic roll and its three results.
3. Rank 3: **9 / 12 / 15 + Logic**. Greater: **11 / 15 / 20 + Logic**, plus a **maneuver and an area ability for each of the four forms** — Ancient Flame, Roaring Storm (lightning **and dazed**), Living Mountain, Devouring Void.
4. Check the elemental's sheet: **stability equals its rank**, and its **resistance to its own element is rank × 2** (a Rank 3 fire elemental shows fire 6).
5. Summon a **Guardian Spirit**. It now has a **light strike (Warding Blow, 2 / 4 / 6 + Persona)** as well as **Warding Aegis**.
6. **Dismiss** is still a free maneuver on the caster's Abilities tab, and still costs nothing.

## 10. Rally to Me (Rally the Crew)

Set up: a Commander, two friendly PCs within 5 squares, one friendly PC **6+ squares away**, one hostile token adjacent to the Commander. Put **Dazed** *and* **Prone** on one ally, **Hidden** on another.

1. Use **Rally the Crew** (5 Influence). The **existing rally sound** must still play.
2. Automatically, for the Commander **and** every friendly token within **5 squares**:
   - the **worst** negative condition is removed — the ally with Dazed + Prone loses **Dazed only** and stays Prone;
   - **Hidden is not touched**, and neither is cover or a Marked edge;
   - each player gets a **"Spend an immediate recovery?"** dialog *on their own screen*. Yes spends one Recovery and heals by that character's Recovery value;
   - **+2 temporary Stamina**. Anyone already above 2 **keeps the higher number**;
   - an **edge on their next power roll**, shown as a status on the token.
3. The **hostile** token and the ally **6 squares away** get nothing.
4. One **chat summary card** appears listing every token and what happened to it (condition removed, recovery spent / declined / none left, temp Stamina, edge).
5. **Offline owner:** log a player out and Rally again — the **Director** gets that character's prompt instead, and the card says so.
6. **No recoveries:** set an ally to 0 Recoveries and Rally — **no dialog opens**, and the card says "no Recoveries left".
7. Have a rallied hero make **any power roll**. It rolls with an edge, and the edge status **disappears** afterwards. The roll after that is normal.

## 11. Lay on Hands

1. Play a **Street Priest**. Target another player's hero and use **Lay On Hands**.
2. That player gets **"Lay on Hands — spend a recovery?"** on their screen, immediately. Yes spends one Recovery and heals them by their Recovery value.
3. A chat card records what they chose.
4. **Offline owner** → the Director is asked. **No recoveries** → no dialog, a message instead.
5. The card's **Conviction spend options still work** on top (extra targets, ending an effect, standing up, temp Stamina).
6. The Lay On Hands sound is unchanged.

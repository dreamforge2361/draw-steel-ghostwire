## Part 1 — Shared ritual rules

### What a Ritual Working is

Your encounter abilities are the fight toolkit. A **Ritual Working** is long-form big magic: a chain of downtime **Projects** that spend yen, study, reagents, and the Veil's attention. In exchange they do things encounter casting never cheaply does — ward a flat overnight, bind a named spirit to your safehouse, open a path between two thin places, carve a relic.

Rituals happen **between runs** or in a **dedicated session beat** (a sealing night, a climax scene). They never replace the fight toolkit.

### The resource firewall (every Working)

- Rituals **never** spend **Essence**, **Conviction**, or **Resonance** — not to find, learn, gather, build, seal, maintain, or keep a payoff alive in a fight. Those pools are encounter casting only.
- Ritual costs are: **¥**, **reagents**, **downtime** (project slots and Project progress), **Price** (personal debt, spirit-attention, favor owed — fiction, not pool points), and **Malice** ticks for the Director.
- A Technomancer's **Resonance Price** on a Wire-rite means narrative attention and Incursion heat. It is **not** spending the Resonance pool.

The **Combat translation** line on every card says what the Working does when a fight breaks out. None of them charge a pool.

### Who may lead, who may help

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| **Leader** | Must have **learned** the Working. Normally an **Elementalist**, **Street Priest**, or **Technomancer** (Wire-rites and Wire skins). The Director may allow an NPC cult leader. |
| **Assistants** | Any hero, casters or not. Grant edges on the study or sealing roll, or Project progress, as the card or Director allows. |
| **Fund only** | A crew with no caster can hire an NPC leader who knows the Working (¥ plus favor or debt). |
| **Barred** | **Cyborgs** can never lead (Arcane Severance). They can assist with hands, muscle, and money. |

Each card names its leaders:
- **General** — Elementalist, Street Priest, or Technomancer. When a Technomancer leads, the Working takes its **Wire skin** (circuit-chalk for salt, datachip for scroll, daemon for spirit) unless the card says otherwise.
- **Elementalist only**, **Street Priest only**, **Technomancer only** — just that class.

### Magnitude

**Magnitude** measures how big a Working is, from 1 to 5. It sets study time, Project goals, sanctum rating, detection range, and how hard the Working is to counter. Spell it out at the table — "Magnitude 3", not an abbreviation.

| Magnitude | Earliest leader echelon | Example payoffs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Echelon 1 | Ward a room overnight, a session charm, a watcher for hours |
| 2 | Echelon 1 | Charm a street focus, remote viewing through a spotter, seal a flat |
| 3 | Echelon 2 | Permanent Restricted item, block-scale ward, a short thin-path |
| 4 | Echelon 3 | Prototype relic, district gateway, bind a named spirit to a site |
| 5 | Echelon 4 / campaign | Apex gate, unmake a major seal (heavy Director veto) |

Sealing also needs a **sanctum rating** at least equal to the Working's Magnitude (see Sanctum).

**Wire-rites** use this same Magnitude ladder. There is no separate Resonance track.

**Magnitude ranges.** Some cards list a range (for example "Magnitude 2–3"). Each Formula Item copy is written at **one** Magnitude inside that range; the Director sets it when the Item is found. That copy's Magnitude sets study time, study goal, and sanctum. A stronger copy is a new find and a new study.

**"Magnitude equal to or greater than the original."** Unmaking Workings that scrub or break something must meet or beat the target's Magnitude. The Formula copy's Magnitude is the ceiling of what it can scrub.

### Veil versus Wire (scope)

A Veil Working touches **spirits** and **Magic-keyword / Veil** targeting. It does **not** touch Wire-only things: sprites, Agents, Watchdog and other ICE, or pure Overlay / Jacked-In Matrix verbs with no Magic keyword.

- **Veil wards do not stop Wire-only intrusion.** To keep sprites, Agents, and ICE out of a room or node, use a Wire-rite ward: **Data Ward**.
- A Wire-rite ward does not stop spirits. Crews who fear both lay both.
- Wire skins of General Workings keep the Working's scope unless the card says the skin changes it.

Each card's **Scope notes** line says which side of this line it sits on.

### The Formula Item: find, learn, cast

Every Working lives on a **Formula Item** on a character sheet. The Item's description **is** the full card — everything in that Working's entry below.

| Form | Feel | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **Datachip / job-stick** | Digital formula, Wire-native, easy to copy wrong | Wire-rites; Wire skins of General Workings |
| **Ritual scroll** | A single rite on a fragile sheet | Magnitude 1–3 General and tradition Workings |
| **Tome** (grimoire, bound volume) | Thick, detailed, often **bulky** in inventory | Magnitude 3–5, Callings, Thresholds, charters |

1. **Find** the Formula Item once — a data steal, a faction or organization archive, a mentor's gift, a stolen grimoire, a thin-place vision. Formula Items are **not** sold at ordinary vendors. Reagents may be; the Formula is not.
2. **Learn** it (study, below). Owning the Item does not let you cast. The Director marks it **learned** for you when study completes.
3. **Cast** only with the Formula Item (or a verified copy) present at sealing **and** the learned mark.

### Projects, in one paragraph

A Project is a downtime job with a **goal** (a number). Each Project roll uses one **project slot** from your Lifestyle band (Squatter 1, Low 1 or 2 with a hireling, Middle 2, High 3, Elite 4 per respite). Make the Power Roll the Project names, then add **progress**: **low = 1**, **middle = 2**, **high = 3**. The Project is done when progress meets the goal.

### The five stages

Every Working runs the same chain. Only some stages are Projects.

| Stage | Is it a Project? | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Study** | **Yes** | **Magnitude × 2** |
| 2. **Components** | **Usually not** — pay the ¥ total and run any fetch beats | — |
| 3. **Sanctum** | **Only when a built sanctum is needed** | **Magnitude × 3** |
| 4. **Sealing** | One collaborative Power Roll | — |
| 5. **Payoff** | The effect lands | — |

A card may run a stage as a Project where the shared rules don't; it prints that stage's goal. **Seal the Flat** runs study (goal 4), sanctum (goal 6), and sealing (goal 4) all as Projects — it is the worked example of the full chain.

#### 1. Study

After you find the Formula Item, you study it.

**Study time** is the **least** time study can take:

| Magnitude | Study time |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 day |
| 2 | 3 days |
| 3 | 1 week |
| 4 | 2 weeks |
| 5 | 1 month |

**Study goal = Magnitude × 2** (Magnitude 1 = 2, Magnitude 2 = 4, Magnitude 3 = 6, Magnitude 4 = 8, Magnitude 5 = 10).

Roll **Logic** or **Instinct**. The Rituals, Occult, Religion, or Matrix Theory skill (Matrix Theory for Wire-rites) gives its usual benefit. Assistants with those skills may grant an edge. Study finishes when the study time has passed **and** progress meets the goal. If the time is up and the goal isn't, keep spending project slots until it is. A bad roll never loses the Formula; it just costs slots.

#### 2. Components

Components are usually **not** a Project. The Formula Item lists:

- each **shoppable** reagent with its ¥ price,
- a **Components total (¥)** — subtract it from crew funds in one hit,
- each **fetch** reagent, marked **¥ —** (no shop price; you get it on a mission),
- **optional** reagents, marked optional and **not** in the total.

Shoppable reagents still respect Availability. Hunting a Restricted reagent can eat a project slot; buying street reagents usually doesn't.

**Pricing bands.** Every card below prints concrete ¥ estimates. When the Director writes a new Formula, or a copy at a different Magnitude, set its Components total inside the band for its Magnitude:

| Magnitude | Components total (¥) band |
|---|---|
| 1 | ¥40–150 |
| 2 | ¥150–500 |
| 3 | ¥500–2,000 |
| 4 | ¥2,000–8,000 |
| 5 | ¥8,000–25,000 |

Extra reagents beyond the list may grant an edge on sealing or blunt Price.

#### 3. Sanctum

You need a working space with a **sanctum rating** at least equal to the Working's Magnitude.

- **Temporary sanctum (default).** Claim the room, rooftop, or thin place for the sealing. No Project. Takes about a day per Magnitude to prepare (it can run alongside study). Lasts until the next dawn or dusk, or until one sealing completes. Rushing it costs more reagents. A temporary sanctum can reach rating **3** at most.
- **Built sanctum (when needed).** A permanent crew or world asset. Build it as a Project: **goal = Magnitude × 3** (rating 4 = 12, rating 5 = 15; rating 3 = 9 when a card calls for a built one). Roll Logic, Instinct, or Persona with a fitting skill; construction trades may assist. Needs up-front ¥ for materials and ongoing Lifestyle upkeep; your Lifestyle band caps how good a sanctum an address can support (`26`).
- **Build once, use many times.** A built sanctum you already own at the right rating costs no new Project.

Under this cap, **every Magnitude 4 or 5 Working needs a built sanctum** at that rating (or a site the card names).

#### 4. Sealing

The leader and anyone helping commit a downtime block (Magnitude 1–2: an evening; Magnitude 3+: a night or a short climax scene). The Formula Item must be present and learned; components are consumed.

Make **one collaborative Power Roll** with the leader's tradition characteristic:
- **Street Priest:** Persona
- **Elementalist:** Logic
- **Technomancer:** Logic (Matrix Theory applies)

Assistants grant edges. Results:

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Low** | Partial payoff, a **hung ritual** (a time bomb the Director holds), or hostile attention. Price resolves as printed. |
| **Middle** | Payoff lands. Price resolves as printed. |
| **High** | Payoff lands clean. Blunt one step of Price (Director says what that means for the card). |

Then **Price** resolves and **spirit-attention** ticks — about equal to Magnitude, fed to the Director as Malice.

**Interrupted mid-seal:** backlash on the leader and assistants, components lost, and a Malice tick.

#### 5. Payoff

The effect lands. Temporary sanctums are spent; built sanctums remain with upkeep. Watch the card's duration and counter.

### Detection — the buildup is a clock

Each stage after Study leaks **Veil pressure** about equal to Magnitude (Director track).

| Magnitude | Leak range |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | **District** — local casters, thin-place sensitives, nearby Overlay |
| 3 | **Hive whisper** — sensitive casters across the Reach may notice |
| 4–5 | **Hive-wide** — Concord, corps, rival ritualists; faction clocks move |

- Quiet study and shopping: little leak. Sanctum raised: detectable per the table. **Sealing night is loud** — nearby casters get a free notice check.
- Overlay / Jacked In gives an edge to notice **Wire-rites** in the district. Citywide Overlay detection only at Magnitude 4+.
- A successful notice reveals: a Working is underway, rough Magnitude band, tradition flavor, rough direction and distance. Not the rite's name unless the result is high or the noticer already knows the Formula.

### Countermagic

There is no free dispel.

- **Before sealing:** spoil or steal components; break the sanctum; feed the leader a false Formula (bane on sealing); scare, bribe, or arrest the assistants.
- **During sealing — Counterseal:** any Elementalist or Street Priest in the scene may spend encounter resource (typically 3+; this is **their** encounter action, not the ritual's cost) and make a contested Power Roll against the leader. Success aborts or corrupts the payoff; failure feeds backlash. Technomancers and Hackers use a **Wired spoof** (Scrambler or hostile ICE into the glyph-layer): Logic with Hacking, contested, same stakes.
- **After payoff:** Unmaking Workings, or destroy or unbond the Item. Every standing effect prints a **Counter** line.

Price and spirit-attention already paid stay paid, even if a counter wins.

### How to read a card

- **Header:** Family · Magnitude · Who may lead · Formula Item form.
- **Payoff** — what it does. **Duration / upkeep** — how long, and what keeps it going (never a pool).
- **Scope notes** — Veil or Wire, and what it can't touch.
- **Combat translation** — what it does when knives come out. No Essence, Conviction, or Resonance.
- **Study time** by Magnitude.
- **Project Points (stages)** — study goal, components, sanctum (and sealing, when the card runs it as a Project).
- **Components** — each reagent with its Availability and ¥, fetch reagents at **¥ —**, optional reagents marked and left out of the **Components total**.
- **Counter** — what breaks or ends it.

## General Workings

*Leaders: Elementalist, Street Priest, or Technomancer (Wire skin). Cyborgs never lead. General cards the older samples gave only to Elementalists and Street Priests (Street-Focus Charm, Watcher, Site Bind) are General here: a Technomancer leads them in the Wire skin.*

### Ward the Room
**Ward** · **Magnitude 1** · **General**

**Formula Item form:** ritual scroll (Veil path) · datachip (Wire-chalk skin)

**Payoff:** One room gets a **Veil barrier**. Spirits and Magic-keyword / Veil remote targeting take a **bane** to enter or pierce it. People, bullets, and ordinary sensors pass as normal — it is not a wall. This is the teaching floor of the Ward family; bigger wards cover more space, last longer, or bite harder.

**Duration / upkeep:** Until the next dawn (or dusk, if sealed at night — pick at sealing). No upkeep.

**Scope notes:** Veil only — spirits and Magic-keyword peeks. Does **not** hinder sprites, Agents, ICE, or pure Overlay / Jacked-In verbs, even with the Wire-chalk skin. For Wire protection lay **Data Ward**.

**Combat translation:** Ambient bane on Veil verbs into or through the room. No summon, no wall against bullets. If a fight starts inside, the ward keeps working on Veil verbs until broken. No pool spend.

**Study time:** 1 day.

**Project Points (stages):**
- **Study goal:** 2.
- **Components:** not a Project — pick one path. **Components total ¥75 (Veil path) / ¥40 (Wire-chalk skin)** (table below).
- **Sanctum:** temporary — the room itself (¥0). No Project.

**Components**

| Component | Path | Availability | ¥ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blessed salt (threshold line) | Veil | Street | 25 |
| Resin incense | Veil | Street | 50 |
| Circuit-chalk (glyph dust) | Wire-chalk skin | Street | 40 |
| Thin-place ash *(optional)* | any | Fetch / mission | — |
| **Components total — Veil path** | | | **¥75** |
| **Components total — Wire-chalk skin** | | | **¥40** |

**Sealing:** one downtime evening. **Price:** negligible; spirit-attention +1 if sealed in a thin place.

**Counter:** Break the salt line or chalk circle; Unmaking at Magnitude 1; dawn.

### Seal the Flat
**Ward** · **Magnitude 2** · **General**

**Formula Item form:** ritual scroll (Veil path) · datachip (Wire-chalk skin)

*Seal the Flat is the full Project prototype: it is the one Magnitude 2 card that runs **study, sanctum, and sealing** as Projects, so a crew can see every stage move on a progress track.*

**Payoff:** A whole flat — every room, door, window, and vent of one dwelling (a Flats unit, a safehouse floor, a coffin-cube row up to about 6 rooms) — gets a **Veil barrier**. Spirits and Magic-keyword / Veil remote targeting take a **bane** to enter or pierce **any** part of it. The first time something Veil-side pushes through (wins past the bane), the leader wakes or knows, wherever they are in the district. People, bullets, and ordinary sensors pass as normal — it is not a wall.

**Duration / upkeep:** Until the **third dawn** after sealing. **Renewal:** before that dawn, re-lay the thresholds (¥60 of blessed salt, one evening, no roll) for three more dawns. Miss a renewal and the seal lapses; start again from the sealing stage (the sanctum Project stays done while the crew lives there).

**Scope notes:** Veil only. Does **not** hinder sprites, Agents, ICE, or pure Overlay / Jacked-In verbs, even with the Wire-chalk skin. For Wire protection lay **Data Ward** on the flat's node too.

**Combat translation:** Ambient bane on Veil verbs into or through any part of the flat. No summon, no wall against bullets. If a fight starts inside, the seal keeps working until a counter breaks it. No pool spend.

**Study time:** 3 days.

**Project Points (stages):**
- **Study goal:** **4** (Project). Logic or Instinct; Rituals, Occult, or Religion (Matrix Theory for the Wire-chalk skin) gives its usual benefit.
- **Components:** not a Project. **Components total ¥180 (Veil path) / ¥150 (Wire-chalk skin)** (table below).
- **Sanctum:** **goal 6** (Project) — the flat itself becomes the sanctum. Each roll cleanses and chalks one or two rooms: scrub the old stains out, map every opening, bless the corners. Roll Instinct, Logic, or Persona with Rituals, Occult, or Religion; anyone who lives there may assist (edge). Once done, the flat stays a rating 2 sanctum while the crew keeps living there — later Seal the Flat castings skip this stage.
- **Sealing:** **goal 4** (Project) — one threshold at a time, over evenings. Each sealing roll uses the leader's tradition characteristic (Persona for a Street Priest; Logic for an Elementalist; Logic with Matrix Theory for a Technomancer). The roll that reaches the goal sets the outcome (low / middle / high, see Sealing in the shared rules). Components are consumed on the first sealing roll.

**Components**

| Component | Path | Availability | ¥ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blessed salt (every threshold, window, and vent line) | Veil | Street | 60 |
| Resin incense (one stick per room) | Veil | Street | 50 |
| Lintel wax seals (four) | Veil | Street | 40 |
| Ward-thread (window and vent cords) | Veil | Street | 30 |
| Circuit-chalk (three tins) | Wire-chalk skin | Street | 120 |
| Conductive tape (window and vent lines) | Wire-chalk skin | Street | 30 |
| Thin-place ash *(optional; edge on the final sealing roll)* | any | Fetch / mission | — |
| A key or lease from the flat's owner *(optional; edge on the sanctum Project)* | any | Fetch / mission | — |
| **Components total — Veil path** | | | **¥180** |
| **Components total — Wire-chalk skin** | | | **¥150** |

**Price:** light — spirit-attention +2 on the final sealing roll (+3 if the flat sits on a thin place). The neighbors smell incense for a week.

**Counter:** Break the salt line at **every** opening, or scrub the lintel seals; Unmaking at Magnitude 2; the third dawn without a renewal. Breaking one threshold opens only that doorway or window — the rest of the flat stays sealed.

**Story walkthrough — Vessa seals the Ninth-and-Fold flat**

1. **The need.** After a loud job, the crew holes up in Vessa's two-room flat above Ninth-and-Fold. Something from the job followed them — a cold draft that moves against the wind, a mirror that fogs from the inside. Ward the Room would cover one room for one night. Vessa wants the whole flat, for days.
2. **Find.** No stall sells this. A dying Shepherd deacon leaves Vessa a folded **scroll** of *Seal the Flat* in a hymnal. It goes on her sheet. It's loot until she studies it.
3. **Study (Project, goal 4).** The Formula's study time is 3 days. Vessa rolls Instinct with Religion over two respites: a middle result (2), then another middle (2). Four progress, three days passed — the Director marks the Formula **learned**.
4. **Components (not a Project).** The crew buys the Veil path at a Flats botanica: salt, incense, wax, thread. **¥180** comes off the crew fund in one hit.
5. **Sanctum (Project, goal 6).** The flat has to be clean before it can be sealed. Vessa scrubs, chalks, and blesses room by room. The crew's Operator lives there too and assists (edge). Rolls: high (3), middle (2), low (1) — six progress. The flat is now a rating 2 sanctum.
6. **Sealing (Project, goal 4).** Over two evenings, Vessa seals the thresholds: the front door and hall window first (Persona, middle — 2), then the back window and the bathroom vent (Persona with an assist, high — 3). Progress 5 meets the goal; the final roll was high, so the payoff lands clean and the Director blunts the Price to spirit-attention +1.
7. **Payoff.** Until the third dawn, anything Veil-side takes a bane to push into any part of the flat. On the second night the cold draft hits the salt at the hall window, fails, and fades. The next evening Vessa spends ¥60 on salt to renew.
8. **What breaks it.** A rival kicks the front-door line in: that door is open to spirits, the rest of the flat stays sealed. Or dawn number three with no renewal.

### Street-Focus Charm
**Artifice** · **Magnitude 2** · **General**

**Formula Item form:** ritual scroll or slim chapbook

**Payoff:** Bonds a short-run charm to one focus, light weapon, or deck-adjacent focus the leader already owns — elemental stain, pact sheen, or Wire varnish by tradition. One named Magic (or Resonance) ability gains an **edge** when used through that Item, **or** the Item gains a one-off Veil tag.

**Duration / upkeep:** One session, or until the next major respite (Director picks at sealing). No upkeep. Charm-tier only; no permanent version.

**Scope notes:** Works on the ability named at sealing; the tradition matches the leader. Doesn't create new abilities.

**Combat translation:** Gear flag — edge on the named ability. No pool spend to keep it. It fades when the duration ends, even mid-fight.

**Study time:** 3 days.

**Project Points (stages):**
- **Study goal:** 4.
- **Components:** not a Project — pay the total from crew funds and run the fetch beats. **Components total ¥250** (table below).
- **Sanctum:** temporary. No Project.

**Components**

| Component | Availability | ¥ |
|---|---|---|
| Street varnish, prayer-oil, or signal lacquer (by tradition) | Professional | 180 |
| Binding thread | Street | 40 |
| Consecration candle | Street | 30 |
| Personal blood or handle smear (Director heat) *(optional)* | Fetch / mission | — |
| **Components total** | | **¥250** |

**Price:** light fatigue or a minor Taint mark until next respite.

**Counter:** Unbond (Unmaking at Magnitude 2); destroy the Item.

### Remote Sense
**Reach** · **Magnitude 2** · **General**

**Formula Item form:** datachip (Wire-native) or ritual scroll

**Payoff:** For one scene, the leader perceives through a **spotter** — an Overlay or Jacked-In ally, a tagged camera, or a minor spirit's eye — within the printed Reach or Wired range.

**Duration / upkeep:** One scene after sealing, or until the link is cut.

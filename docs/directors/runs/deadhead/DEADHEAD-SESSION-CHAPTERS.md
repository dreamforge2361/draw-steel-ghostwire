## Opening — Why tonight

Ossian Reach is Veyra's crown-jewel hive: a vertical city that climbs from the Halo down through the Spires, the Grid, the Flats, the Warrens, and the Sinks. Twelve Conglomerates own the sky and the feeds. The Wired is the second street — always on, always listening. Down in the Flats, reputations are currency and ghosts work off the ledger. It is somewhere around 2050, and the cover stories have stopped adding up.

Tonight is not a Quiet Floor job. Tonight is **Deadhead**.

Mama Cassavir of the Switchboard has work that needs deniable hands: lift a **live Argent Exchange (ARG) transaction wafer** — street slang: a **ghost ledger mirror**, a live portable copy of ARG money-motion — from a sealed courier capsule riding the **Gold Line** cargo maglev on its run from Spire depot to Switchboard terminus. Board mid-canyon on a borrowed trash freighter. Crack the capsule on the Wire. Bail on the same bird before ARG answers the forced stop. Baseline pay is eight thousand if the prize comes home to Mama; other doors exist only as rumor weather. The briefing chip she will hand you is **Mama's Deadhead Job Stick (Gold Line)**. It is not the prize. The prize is the wafer still nested in its capsule, somewhere on that silver freight spine.

Four runners. One club door. A bird that is not theirs yet. And a maglev that will wipe the ledger mirror if anyone is foolish enough to emergency-stop the consist while the wafer is still nested.

Read the Scenes in order. **Spoken table narration** lives in **READ ALOUD — Meatspace** and **READ ALOUD — Wired** boxes. Foundry pointers, dials, If pressed, roll cues, and checklists wait at the end of each Scene under **Director Notes** (or a short ***Director*** line after a box).

**Power Rolls:** low ≤11 / middle 12–16 / high 17+ — full engine under **Mama's Club → Rules reminder** (characteristics `02`; Wire verbs `21`). Later Scenes keep unique procedures in their Director Notes only.

## Scene — Mama's Club

> **READ ALOUD — Meatspace**
>
> Mama's Club sits in the Flats like a Switchboard confession booth with a stage license. For years it has been the meet-house where Torcs hold reputation, where favors get priced before they get spoken, and where Mama Cassavir takes her cut without raising her voice. The street knows the door: cracked industrial alley, green pipework, steel panels bleeding neon warmth. Inside, yellow stage spots and a gold L-bar neon sign do the hospitality. #### Rules reminder — Mama's Club

**Power Roll engine (fingertips):** **2d10 + characteristic** → **low ≤11 / middle 12–16 / high 17+**. Characteristics: **Physique · Reflex · Logic · Instinct · Persona**. Skills usually **+2**. Edges and banes cancel (one each cancel; leftovers become edge / double edge / bane / double bane — roll extra d10s and drop lowest or highest). Difficulty shortcut: **Easy** = middle+ succeeds; **Medium** = high succeeds, middle = costly/incomplete; **Hard** = high succeeds (middle costly/incomplete; often apply a bane). Natural double (matching faces on the first two dice) = critical cue.

**Negotiation process (full — do not flip to `03` mid-brief):**

1. Director names the NPC’s **drive** (Greed, Higher Authority, Freedom, Vengeance, Fear, or a one-line want) and one **pitfall** (the line that tanks the deal).
2. The talking runner makes an opening **Persona** Power Roll (+ **Negotiation** skill if they have it → usually +2). Low / middle / high move the terms: pay band, access, heat.
3. Each additional argument is **another** Power Roll, a spent heroic resource, or a hero token. After **two** failures (lows that fail the ask), or if someone hits the **pitfall**, the deal sours — walk or fight.
4. Class text that names a matching motivation (Freedom / Greed / Vengeance, etc.) grants an **edge** when the fiction matches that drive.

**Tonight vs Mama (apply the process above):** Drive **Greed** (or Higher Authority / Switchboard clean). Difficulty **Hard**. Outcome bands are in Director Notes above (Low = no raise; Middle = ¥9k or ¥1k up front; High = ¥10k or ¥8k + soft favor; Cap = no chamber / Trace / wipe exceptions).

**Pitfalls (one hit tanks the deal — Job Stick still offered at baseline, or they walk):**
- Demanding the **exact capsule chamber** (“Which car? Second-to-last? Give us the seat number.”).
- Asking her to put **her face / Switchboard / Torc** on the train, or joking that ARG will “know who sent us.”
- Treating Switchboard deniability as optional: “Just say we work for Mama,” “Put your name on the hail,” “Wire us through the club booth.”
- Insulting the chrome/human gap: mocking the late smile, calling her a toaster, or demanding she “act normal.”

**Edge — grant when fiction matches (max one edge from this list unless a class motivation also matches Greed / Higher Authority):**

*Cyborg / human gap (they answer the voice she is using):*
- Match her **clip** with clipped runner talk — short asks, no soft soap, treat the Job Stick like a work order.
- Match her **forced maternal** beat without mocking it — accept the “loves / forgive the manners” moment as real-enough, then return to business.
- Name the gap kindly and usefully: “You’re machine when it matters; we’re ghosts when yours can’t be. That’s the hire.”
- After a successful **Optional Insight**, quote the tell in play (“Your smile lagged — we’re still in.”) and pivot to terms.

*Deniability (they protect her booth and Switchboard clean):*
- Open with ghosts, not agency: “No booth smell. No Torc. No ARG ticket with your face.”
- Offer concrete deniable cover of their own (false hail, cutouts, “we never met”) instead of asking her for cover.
- Frame the raise as **risk premium for staying deniable** (“Eight is thin if we keep your name off the Wire”) — not as a threat to burn her.
- Accept the wipe rule / no-chamber wall without fishing for booth-killing intel, then push ¥ only.

**No edge for:** generic “please,” pure intimidation, or any line that hits a pitfall above.

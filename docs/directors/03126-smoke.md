## Decisions this build made, and why

Three places the brief left a choice. All three are locked in the smoke, so they cannot drift.

### 1. The 3-Pack is one row of quantity 3, not a container

`raw-reagent-3-pack.json` carries `system.quantity: 3` and **byte-identical `consumableUse` block**
to the single. Buying it puts one row on the sheet that depletes 3 → 2 → 1 → 0.

*Why:* it keeps **one Use path**. A pack-as-container would need a second ability ("open the case"),
a second SKU to spawn, and a kiosk price on a thing that is not a dose — three moving parts to save
the player one drag. The only wart is cosmetic: a case with one ampoule left still reads
"Raw Reagent (3-Pack) ×1". The SKU's own copy says "one case, quantity 3" so nobody is surprised.

### 2. Family → folder mapping

Stamped on every SKU as `flags.draw-steel-ghostwire.gear.ammoFamily`, mirrored by
`AMMO_FAMILY_BY_DSID` in `scripts/ammo.mjs`. The smoke re-derives the whole table from the tree on
every run, so a new gun dropped in a folder that is not stamped fails the smoke rather than silently
spending nothing.

| Family | Capacity | Folder | SKUs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Handgun** | **12** | `weapons/light-firearms/` | ghost-pistol · hand-cannon · popper · sleeve-gun · slugger · workhorse · zapper · **chatter** |
| **SMG** | **30** | `weapons/light-firearms/` | streetsweeper-smg · buzz-gun |
| **Longarm** | **20** | `weapons/longarms/` | apex-rifle · brush-gun · chopper · longshot · milspec-battle-rifle · pipe-rifle · streetline-carbine · whisper-rifle |
| **Shotgun** | **8** | `weapons/longarms/` | autoshotgun · boomstick |
| **Heavy** | **50** | `weapons/heavy/` | chatterbox · dragons-breath · grease-gun · hand-of-god · siege-missile · tank-cracker · wallbreaker |

Two rows are deliberate and easy to get backwards:

- **Chatter is a Handgun.** The lock's Handgun row names it; its own catalog blurb calls it a
  "suppressed SMG". The enumerated list is the lock, the blurb is flavour — but if Michael meant SMG,
  this is the one line to flip (`chatter.json` → `"ammoFamily": "smg"`, plus the table in
  `scripts/ammo.mjs` and `SMG_DSIDS` in the smoke).
- **buzz-gun is an SMG** even though its blurb says "machine pistol", for the same reason in reverse.

**Out of scope, and verified out:** melee, `bows-exotic/`, `thrown/` and `weapons/mounted/` spend no
rounds at all. **A gun on a §5F hardpoint spends nothing either** — `spendsAmmo()` exempts anything
with `flags.<module>.mount.mountedOn` or carried by a deployed machine Actor, so 0.3.112 Mounted fire
is untouched this pass.

### 3. Inert Ammo-shelf consolidation

The Ammo Counter is now **exactly the three round types**. The three inert grenades on it had no Use
and no way to get one:

| Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `general/ammunition/frag-grenade.json` | **deleted** | exact duplicate of `weapons/thrown/frag.json`, which is the usable one |
| `general/ammunition/flash-bang.json` | **deleted** | exact duplicate of `weapons/thrown/flash-bang-3e.json` |
| `general/ammunition/smoke.json` | **moved** to `weapons/thrown/smoke-grenade.json` | Smoke had no thrown twin, so rather than delete a SKU with nothing to replace it, it *became* the thrown one |

Smoke keeps its `_id` (`5VR1vIjajQKweeEJ`) and its `_dsid` (`smoke`), so a sheet that already holds
one is unaffected — it just gained a Throw. Its three lang entries (`FragGrenade`, `FlashBang`,
`Smoke`) went with the SKUs; Smoke's copy lives under `SmokeGrenade` now.

## A · Raw Reagent

Buy from a **Street Clinic / Medical** kiosk, or drag from **Gear → Medical**.

- [ ] A Medical kiosk lists **Raw Reagent** at **¥2,000** and **Raw Reagent (3-Pack)** at **¥5,500**,
      on the same shelf as Trauma Patch / Stim Patch / Field Surgery Kit / Antidote Dose / Slap-Doc Kit.
- [ ] Put one on a **Medic**. A **Use Raw Reagent** maneuver appears on their sheet.
- [ ] Their Reagent pool is below capacity. Use it: the pool goes **up by 1**, the chat card says
      *"Kit restocked: +1 Reagent (now N)"*, and the ampoule is spent (quantity → 0).
- [ ] Set their pool to **one below capacity** and use another. It fills to the ceiling and the card
      says *"kit capacity, so it stops at N"*.
- [ ] Set their pool to **capacity**. Using it **refuses** ("kit is already at capacity") and **no
      card posts** and **nothing is spent**.
- [ ] Put one on a **non-Medic** (any other class). Using it **refuses** with
      *"… is not a Medic — Raw Reagent has no kit to go into"*, **no card posts**, nothing spent.
- [ ] Put the **3-Pack** on a Medic. It is **one row at quantity 3**. Use it three times: the pool
      goes up 1 each time and the row counts down to 0.
- [ ] The pool never exceeds the echelon ceiling (10 / 14 / 20 / 38, +2 with Advanced Chem-Prep) —
      and the Director sheet-typing clamp from 0.3.125 still bites.

## B · Gun ammunition

Put a **Popper** (handgun, 12), a **Streetsweeper SMG** (30) and a box of **Standard Rounds** on a hero.

### Reload

- [ ] A **Reload** maneuver appears on the sheet as soon as a gun that takes ammo is on it, and
      **disappears** when the last one is removed.
- [ ] Standard Rounds show **quantity 30** on the sheet. That number is **rounds**, not magazines.
- [ ] Use **Reload** → pick the Popper, pick Standard. The card reads
      *"reloads Popper — 12 Standard Rounds in, 12/12 loaded"* and the stack drops to **18**.
- [ ] Reload again with the Popper full: it **refuses** ("already full of Standard Rounds").
- [ ] Reload the **SMG** with 18 left in the pocket: it takes all **18** (18/30) — a partial fill is
      fine, it is **one action** either way.

### Firing

- [ ] Fire the Popper. The attack card resolves as normal, and carries a line at the bottom:
      **"Standard Rounds — 11/12 left in the Popper."**
- [ ] Empty it (fire twelve times, or set `flags.draw-steel-ghostwire.ammo.count` to 0). The next
      **Fire Popper** refuses with **"You are out of ammunition."** and **no roll card posts**.
- [ ] Reload it and the refusal goes away.

### Switching type

- [ ] Buy **AP Rounds**. With 5 Standard still in the Popper, Reload → Popper → AP Rounds.
- [ ] The Popper is **12/12 AP**, the AP stack drops by **12**, and the **5 Standard go back** to the
      Standard stack (card: *"5 Standard Rounds go back in the pocket"*).
- [ ] With **no** AP in the pocket, a switch to AP **refuses** and the 5 Standard **stay in the gun**.

### Stick-n-Shock

- [ ] Buy **Gel/Stick-n-Shock** and reload with it.
- [ ] Target a token and fire. The card's ammo line reads **Gel/Stick-n-Shock** and adds
      *"Stick-n-Shock: 1 Stamina and Dazed instead of the rolled damage."*
- [ ] The target lost **1 Stamina** and has **Dazed**. **Do not also click Apply Damage** — Gel
      replaces the printed damage, it does not stack with it.

### Mounted is untouched

- [ ] Mount a **Wallbreaker** on a §5F Weaponry kit. Firing it from the chassis **does not** ask for
      ammunition and **does not** refuse. (Hand-carried, it does.)

## C · Grenades and thrown

Put a **Frag**, a **Flash-Bang**, an **EMP Grenade** and a **Smoke** on a hero.

- [ ] A single **Throw Grenade** main action appears — **one shared ability**, not one per grenade.
- [ ] The old single-target **"Fire Frag"** free strike is **gone**. (It is swept on load for sheets
      that already had one.)
- [ ] **Thermite Charge** and **Shaped Charge** still have their Adjacent strikes — they are placed,
      not lobbed.

### The circle

- [ ] Target an enemy token, use **Throw Grenade**, pick Frag. A **20 ft circle** MeasuredTemplate
      lands centred on that token. (On a 5 ft/square map that is 4 squares.)
- [ ] With **nothing** targeted, it asks you to **click** where it lands. **Right-click cancels** the
      throw and nothing is spent.
- [ ] The grenade's quantity drops by **1**.

### The saves

Each token under the circle rolls **2d10 + Reflex (agility)** and the resolution card lists them.

- [ ] **Fail** (total ≤ 11) → the full impact at **your** power-roll tier.
- [ ] **Normal success** (12+) → **nothing**.
- [ ] **Critical success** (natural **19–20** on the save) → **the middle (tier 2) impact anyway**.
      *This is the lock and it is not a bug: a crit is getting most of the way clear, not a clean shrug.*
- [ ] Throw at **tier 3** (or roll a natural 19–20 on the throw) and a token **fails** → the row is
      flagged **HIGH**.

### The impacts

- [ ] **Frag** deals its printed tier damage — 4 / 6 / 8 by your tier.
- [ ] **Flash-Bang** deals **no damage at all** and applies **Dazed**. Its catalog entry no longer
      claims 4 electrical.
- [ ] **EMP Grenade** (¥5,000, Weapons Cage) deals no damage. A failed save **suppresses every
      implant** on the target — their benefits switch off, per the F12 chrome rules.
- [ ] Throw the EMP on a **HIGH** result and a failed save → the card says **2 rounds**, not 1.
      (Soft/bioware chrome is still one step harder to knock down; salvage one step easier — that is
      the standing chrome rule, not an EMP rule. *Suppression duration is announced on the card; the
      chrome state itself has no round counter — clear it with the normal reboot at the start of the
      target's next turn, twice for a HIGH hit.*)
- [ ] **Smoke** places its circle with **no save**: everyone inside gets **Cover/Conceal** (a bane on
      ranged attacks against them). It is on the **Weapons Cage**, not the Ammo Counter.

### The shelves

- [ ] The **Ammo Counter** kiosk lists **exactly three** SKUs: Standard Rounds, AP Rounds,
      Gel/Stick-n-Shock. No grenades.
- [ ] The **Weapons Cage** lists Frag, Flash-Bang, Smart-Grenade, Gasser, Firestarter, **EMP Grenade**,
      **Smoke**, Thermite Charge, Shaped Charge, Throwing Knife.
- [ ] There is **no second buyable Frag** and **no second buyable Flash-Bang** anywhere.

## Known limits of this pass

- **Suppression is not timed.** `scripts/chrome-damage.mjs` has no round counter on the `suppressed`
  state — it is cleared by the normal reboot. The EMP card names the duration; the Director clears it.
- **Gel does not stop Draw Steel's own damage button.** The system's Apply Damage button is still on
  the attack card. The ammo line says in as many words not to click it.
- **Throw does not ask for a target for the save.** Everything whose token centre is inside the
  circle rolls, friend or foe. That is what a grenade does.

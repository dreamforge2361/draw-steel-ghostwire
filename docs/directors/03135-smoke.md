# 0.3.135 — Foundry smoke checklist

Rally edge on ability rolls · Hurl Element / Bolt Barrage colour · VOIDMARK full wave · drone token scale.

**Before you start**

1. Close Foundry, pull `gw-0.3.135`, reopen. The module version in **Manage Modules** should read **0.3.135**.
2. Open the console (F12) on load. There are **no new migrations** in this wave — nothing rewrites your world.
   You should see the usual registration lines; one of them changed wording:
   - `draw-steel-ghostwire | Rally the Crew and Lay On Hands registered (shared recovery prompt; rally edge on ability rolls)`
3. **No art changed.** No drone WebP was generated or replaced and no silhouette plate was touched. The drones
   in item 4 are wearing exactly the art they wore in 0.3.134, drawn at a different size.

Everything below is in brief order. `✅` when it matches, note what you saw when it does not.

## 1. Rally edge — the edge reaches the dice

**What was wrong.** Rally the Crew put a *Rallied* effect on everybody and the effect added 1 to
`system.characteristics.*.edges`. Draw Steel builds an **ability** roll from a different bag
(`config.modifiers.edges`), and characteristic edges are a **test** concept — so the icon appeared on the
sheet, the card said "edge on your next power roll", and every ability roll came out at zero edges. The
Director was applying it by hand or not at all.

**What to check.**

1. Put a **Commander** and one ally on a scene, within 5 squares of each other.
2. Fire **Rally the Crew**. Both sheets get the **Rallied — edge on your next roll** effect, and the summary
   card lists `edge on its next roll` for each row. (All of 0.3.134's Rally behaviour is unchanged: worst
   condition lifted, Recovery prompt on the owner's own screen, +2 non-stacking temporary Stamina.)
3. Now roll an **ability with a power roll** on the rallied ally — any strike will do, e.g. **Fire Ferrum
   Rivet**, or the Commander's own **Commander Mark** follow-up attack.
   - **The roll dialog must show 1 edge already set** before you press Roll. That is the fix: the edge is
     seeded into the dialog where you can *see* it and the Director can clear it, not applied silently after
     the dice have landed.
   - The result card should read as a single-edge roll (a **double edge** if something else — Flanked, a
     Mark, a bench — was also giving one; edges cap at Draw Steel's normal double).
4. **The edge is spent.** Right after that roll, the **Rallied** effect disappears from the sheet and you get
   the toast `<name>'s rally edge is spent.`
5. **A non-roll ability must not eat it.** Rally again, then use a **maneuver with no power roll** (e.g.
   **Take Cover**, **Reload**, **Command**). The maneuver resolves and the **Rallied effect is still there** —
   it is waiting for a roll, which is what the card promises.
6. Rally again and then **cancel** the roll dialog on an ability. Nothing was rolled, so the edge survives.

### 1b. Characteristic and test rolls — what to expect (documented, not changed)

The Active Effect is deliberately still there, and it still adds +1 to all five characteristic edges. So:

- **Ability rolls** get the edge from the explicit `config.modifiers.edges` bump this wave adds. This is the
  one that was broken.
- **Characteristic tests** (a Reason test, a skill test off the sheet) may show the edge from the Active
  Effect, because that is the key a test reads. That is intended and matches the card text — "edge on your
  next power roll" does not distinguish.
- **What must NOT happen** is the same roll getting the edge twice. If you ever see a rallied hero's ability
  roll come out at **two edges from Rally alone** — with no Flank, no Mark, no bench — write it down; that
  would mean the system started reading characteristic edges on ability rolls too.
- Whichever kind of roll spends it, the **first** roll clears the effect. A test and an ability roll cannot
  both benefit from one Rally.

## 2. Hurl Element / Bolt Barrage — the element you picked is the colour you get

**What was wrong.** 0.3.134 read the element off the card's damage type, which is correct, using
`Object.values(power.effects)`. On a *live* ability that property is a **CollectionField**, and
`Object.values` on it returns nothing — so the element came back empty on every real card in a running world
and the FX fell through to the pale-blue arcane default. The attunement write path was never the problem:
chat already said **Fire Damage**.

**What to check.**

1. Open an **Elementalist** (Kaës, or any hero with the class). Right-click any attuned ability row →
   **Attunement** → **fire**. The chat/notification confirms the re-type.
2. Target something across the room and fire **Hurl Element**.
   - The card reads **Fire Damage**.
   - **The effect on the canvas is orange-red with a hot pale core**, and the sound is the fireball. It must
     **not** be the pale blue arcane bolt.
3. Switch attunement to **cold** and fire again. Icy pale blue-white, and clearly a *different* colour from
   the arcane fallback (compare against a non-elemental spell such as a Technomancer card).
4. Fire **Bolt Barrage** (the multi-target one) with fire attuned, then with cold. Same rule: every bolt in
   the volley wears the attunement's colour.
5. Try **acid** (green), **corruption** (violet) and **poison** (sickly green) if you want the full set —
   all nine elements have their own hue.
6. **Everyone at the table sees the same colour.** With a second client connected, the relayed effect is the
   same hue on both screens.
7. **The fallback.** If a Director hand-builds a Hurl Element copy whose damage tiers are untyped, the effect
   now falls back to the **hero's attunement flag** rather than arcane blue. To see it: duplicate Hurl
   Element onto a hero, clear its damage types by hand, keep the hero attuned to fire, and fire it — still
   orange-red.
8. Automated Animations / Sequencer / JB2A stay **optional and off** in `ghostwire-v2`; this is the built-in
   canvas path. Nothing in this wave depends on them.

## 3. VOIDMARK — any card, any order, and a question back when it is unsure

**What was wrong.** VOIDMARK could answer a *chapter* question and not a *card* question. The 418 abilities,
46 ritual Workings and 68 summon Actors were not in the index at all (only 34 summons had a generated
chapter), so "what does Ward the Room do?" came back **"that packet is not on this channel"** for a Working
that ships with four paragraphs of its own text. And 0.3.134's follow-up fix merged the previous turn into one
search string, which meant a summon question three messages back **out-scored the new topic** — the workaround
at the table was hitting **Clear thread** between subjects.

### 3a. Any card, by name

1. Open VOIDMARK (the ghost in the Token controls), **Director** mode.
2. Ask **"What does Ward the Room do?"** — cold, first message of a fresh thread.
   - You get the Working's actual rules: **one room**, a **bane** on spirits and Veil magic aimed in from
     outside, **does not** stop people, bullets, cameras or hackers, expires at the **next dawn**.
   - The citation chips name **Ghostwire Ritual Workings — Ward the Room**.
   - It must **not** say the packet is not on this channel.
3. Ask about three more Workings by name — **Seal the Flat**, **Data Ward**, **Scrub the Ghost-Code**. All 46
   are first-class entries; try any of them.
4. Ask about a plain ability: **"What does Hurl Element do?"**, **"How does Spot Target work?"**,
   **"What does Field Triage cost?"**. You should get the card's own tiers, distance, target and cost.
5. Ask about a summon: **"What can a Zephyr Companion do?"** — Stamina, speed, its **Striking Wind** card and
   its **Command** maneuver.
6. Ask about a machine: **"What is a Mule-Bot?"**, **"What does the Sentry Turret do?"**.

### 3b. No CLEAR THREAD between topics

This is the one Michael asked for by name. **Do not clear the thread at any point in this sequence.**

1. Fresh thread. Ask **"I am an elementalist and I've just summoned my electrical spirit what can he do?"**
2. Let it answer. Ask a follow-up: **"and how much damage?"** — it should still be talking about the same
   creature. (That is 0.3.134's continuity, kept.)
3. Now change the subject completely, in the same thread: **"What does ward the room do?"**
   - You must get **Ward the Room**, same as in 3a, with the Ward citation first.
   - The summon conversation may appear as background, but the answer must be about the ward.
4. Change subject again without clearing: **"How does a combat round work?"**, then **"What is Overlay?"**,
   then back to **"What does Ward the Room do?"**. Every one lands on its own topic.
5. **If you ever have to hit Clear thread to get a straight answer, that is the bug** — write down the exact
   sequence.

### 3c. An Elementalist's electrical spirit is not a pact spirit

1. Fresh thread. Ask **"I am an elementalist and I've just summoned my electrical spirit what can he do?"**
   - The answer is about the **Zephyr Companion** and/or a **Bound Elemental** — the Elementalist's own
     creatures, with their strikes and the Command maneuver.
   - It must **not** describe the **Guardian**, **Hunter** or **Warrior Spirit**. Those are Street Priest pact
     spirits and have nothing to do with an Elementalist.
2. Short form, without saying the class: **"what can my electrical spirit do?"**, **"how does a lightning
   spirit attack?"**, **"electrical zephyr damage"**. Same routing — no pact spirits.
3. The other direction still works: **"I'm a Street Priest, what does my pact spirit do?"** → Guardian /
   Hunter / Warrior, as it should be.
4. **Selected token.** Place an **Electrical Zephyr** (or a **Bound Elemental (Rank 3)**) on the scene,
   **select its token**, and ask just **"what can it do?"** — the answer should be about *that* creature. Then
   select a different summon and ask the same bare question; the answer follows the selection.

### 3d. "Did you mean X?" instead of a guess

1. Ask something deliberately ambiguous: **"what does the ward do?"** (no "the room").
   - VOIDMARK should come back with **one short question** naming the candidates — something like
     *"Did you mean Ward the Room, Data Ward, or Storm Ward?"*
   - It must **not** pick one silently, and it must **not** say the packet is not on this channel.
2. Try **"how does the spike work?"** (Spike Agent Minor / Intermediate / Advanced) and **"what does the
   ritual cost?"**. Same shape of reply.
3. Answer its question (**"Ward the Room"**) and confirm it then gives you the full rules.
4. A question that names its card outright (**"What does Ward the Room do?"**) must **never** ask back.

### 3e. Rules first, then Mark's read

1. Ask a question with a tactical edge: **"My crew is pinned in a stairwell by two Corp Enforcers — what do I
   do?"** or **"is Ward the Room worth a night's work before a Veil job?"**
2. The answer should come in two parts:
   - the **rules**, grounded in the cards and chapters, with numbers exactly as printed; then
   - **Mark's read** / **Tactical angle** — his own inference, perspectives, tactics, ideas.
3. The second part may be opinionated. It must **not** contain an invented number, distance, duration or
   condition. If an idea needs a ruling, he should say it is a Director call.
4. In **Runner** mode the same split happens in voice ("my read") rather than as a labelled heading.

### 3f. Offline check

`node tools/voidmark-smoke.mjs` and `node tools/wave-03135-smoke.mjs` assert all of the above that can be
asserted without a key: the index counts (418 / 46 / 68), Ward-the-Room-first alone **and** after a summon
turn, the electrical-spirit routing, the selected-token boost, and the clarify candidates.

## 4. Drone token scale — three bands, three sizes

**No new art.** Every drone is wearing the plate it already had; only the **art scale** on the token changed.
Token **footprint** (how many grid squares it occupies) is unchanged.

| Band | Item `scale` word | Art scale |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| Personal / **Micro** | `Personal` | **0.5** |
| Light / **Small** | `Light` | **1.0** |
| Vehicle / **Medium** | `Vehicle` | **1.5** |

1. **Compendium → Ghostwire Summons & Machines.** Open **Generic Drone (Micro)** → Prototype Token → Appearance.
   **Scale 0.5**. Then **Generic Drone (Small)** → **1.0**, **Generic Drone (Medium)** → **1.5**.
   Drop all three on a scene side by side: the Micro reads as a palm-sized bug, the Medium as a hauler.
2. **Mule-Bot** (the named Medium drone Actor) → **1.5**, on its own `mule-bot.webp` art.
3. **Deploy one of each band from a hero's inventory** — this is the real test, because deploying stamps a
   fresh Actor every time:
   - **Micro:** a **Skitter**, **Fly**, **Buzz**, **Needle**, **Taser-Bee** or **Tape-Eye** → token art at **0.5**.
   - **Small:** a **Guard-Dog**, **Spotter**, **Crawler**, **Medbot**, **Wrenchbot** or **Rotor** → **1.0**.
   - **Medium:** a **Mule-Bot**, **Warhound**, **Hellkite**, **Razorwing** or **Pallbearer** → **1.5**.
   Check both the placed token's **Appearance → Scale** and how it reads on the map next to a hero.
4. **Vehicles must not move.** Deploy a **Lane-Hopper**, a **Star-Chopper** and a **Bulldog**. Their art scale
   stays whatever it was (**1.0**) — this wave is drones only.
5. **Base assets must not move.** Deploy a **Door Lock**, a **Safehouse Beacon** or a **Workshop Bench**.
   They borrow the small-drone band as a scaffold but are furniture; scale unchanged.
6. **Recall and re-deploy** a Medium drone. Same 1.5 — the stamp is not a one-off.
7. **Existing deployed drones in your world keep the old scale** until you **Recall** and **Deploy** them
   again. There is no migration in this wave (see the result doc); a Recall/Deploy round-trip is the fix, and
   you can always set Scale by hand on a token you do not want to re-deploy.

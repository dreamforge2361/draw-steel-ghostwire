## Combat & resource firewall

Rituals are **between runs** or a **dedicated session beat**. They never replace the fight toolkit.

### Hard bars (global — do not repeat on each Working)
- Ritual Workings **MUST NOT** spend **Essence / Conviction / Resonance** to cast, seal, or keep a payoff alive in combat.
- Those pools stay encounter casting only.
- Ritual costs = **¥**, **reagents**, **downtime / Project Points**, **Veil Price / spirit-attention / Resonance-flavored narrative Price** (fiction debt — not Resonance points), and **Malice ticks**.

### Default combat translations
- **Watcher / nest:** alert fiction or **one free notice** when the trip fires. Not a full combat summon unless an upgraded Calling Working says so.
- **Long Leash / Site Bind / True-Name Collar (Deep Bind):** use **§C3** independent Stamina **or** extension rules when knives come out. A ritual bind is a **site/crew asset**, not free encounter-summon spam. Prefer **no** resource spend to keep it on the board; optional Persistent only if the table already uses class-summon Persistent math for that entity type.
- **Wards:** ambient **bane / edge** on the environment. No caster resource drip unless a separate Persistent working is chosen **in combat** from the class sheet.
- **Artifice items:** gear flags on an Item. No upkeep resource in the fight.
- **Wire-rites:** same firewall. **Resonance Price** = narrative attention / Incursion heat — **not** spending the Resonance pool to maintain the Working.

### Formula Item & Learning

Every Ritual Working requires a **Foundry Formula Item** on a character sheet — one of:

| Form | Feel | Typical Mag band |
|---|---|---|
| **Datachip / job-stick** | Digital formula, Wire-native, easy to copy-wrong; still found via Wire jobs, not kiosks | Mag 1–3 Wire-rites; some General Reach/Ward skins |
| **Ritual scroll** | Single-rite sheet, fragile — **found/stolen**, not vendor stock | Mag 1–3 General / tradition |
| **Tome / grimoire / bound volume** | Thick detailed book — often **bulky** inventory | Mag 3–5, Calling, Threshold, Unmaking, charters |

**Find → Learn → Cast**
1. **Find** the Formula Item **once** — data steal, faction/org archive, mentor gift, stolen grimoire, thin-place vision, or similar. **Not** sold at ordinary vendors; these are powerful esoterica / secret knowledge.
2. **Learn** it (Lore stage / study Project). Owning the Item ≠ cast rights until the **learned** flag is set for that hero (or their cabal’s shared lodge, if Exclusive Charter says so).
3. **Cast** only with the Formula Item **(or a verified copy)** present at sealing **and** the learned flag. Reagents may still be shoppable; the Formula itself is not.

**Item-as-full-card (LOCK):** the Foundry Formula Item description holds the **complete** Director+player ritual card — not a stub pointer to this catalog. Minimum card contents:

- What the Working **does** (payoff, duration, impact)
- **Magnitude**, **family**, who may **lead** (General / Elementalist / Street Priest / Technomancer)
- Full **stage outline**: Lore (learn gate) → Components → Sanctum → Sealing → Payoff
- **Components in detail** — named reagents/parts with Availability / ¥ band hints; mark **shoppable** vs **quest/mission fetch** (unique reagent runs)
- **Combat translation** + resource firewall (no Essence / Conviction / Resonance)
- **Counter** condition when relevant

Secrets may use Foundry secret blocks for Director-only Price numbers if needed; prefer **player-facing components** visible on the Item.

Each entry below ends with a **Formula:** line naming typical form + reminding that the Item text **is** the full card.

## 1. General / Any ritual leader

Leaders: Elementalist, Street Priest, or Technomancer (Wire skin where noted). Cyborgs barred from leading.

### G1. Ward the Room [B43]
**(General, Mag 1, Ward)**

- **Does:** Marks one room with a **Veil barrier** (Mag 1). Spirits and Mag remote peeks take a **bane** to enter or pierce; flesh, bullets, and mundane sensors still pass. Mag 1 = room + overnight + ambient bane — the teaching floor of the Ward family, not a physical wall. Higher Mag wards scale space, duration, or hardness.
- **Duration:** Until next dawn (or dusk if sealed at night — pick one at sealing). Temporary sanctum = the room itself.
- **Impact:** Safehouse night, hostage hold, or “don’t peek in here” on a flat. District-quiet Mag 1 leak unless sealed in a thin place.
- **Scope:** Spirits + Magic/Veil remote peeks only. **Not** Wire-only sprites/Agents/ICE or pure Overlay verbs — use Data Ward (Wire-rite) for that.
- **Combat:** Ambient bane on spirits / Mag remote targeting entering or piercing the room. Not a wall vs bullets. No summon token. If a fight starts inside, the ward still applies to Veil verbs until broken.
- **Components (¥ on Formula card):** Blessed salt ¥25 + resin incense ¥50 = **Components total ¥75** (Veil path); **or** circuit-chalk ¥40 = **Components total ¥40** (Wire-skin). Optional thin-place ash: ¥ — (fetch). Subtract the total from party funds when bought.
- **Study time:** 1 day (Magnitude 1).
- **Counter:** Break the circle/salt line; Unmaking Mag 1.
- **Formula:** Ritual scroll (common) or datachip Wire-chalk skin. Item = full ritual card.

### G2. Street-Focus Charm [B43]
**(General, Mag 2, Artifice)**

- **Does:** Bonds a short-run enchantment to one existing focus, light weapon, or deck-adjacent focus. Tradition matches the leader — elemental stain, pact sheen, or Wire resonance varnish. One named Mag (or Resonance) ability rides easier for a session, or the Item gains a one-off Veil tag.
- **Duration:** One session (or until next major respite — Director pick at seal). Permanent SKUs are out of scope; this is charm-tier.
- **Impact:** Prep beat before a hard run; makes one signature trick feel blessed without rewriting the class sheet.
- **Combat:** Gear flag only — edge on the named ability when used. No upkeep spend; charm fades when duration ends even mid-fight.
- **Components:** Focus already owned; street varnish / prayer-oil / signal lacquer (shoppable Restricted-); optional personal blood-or-handle smear (fetch if Director wants heat).
- **Counter:** Unbond Project Mag 2; destroy the item.
- **Formula:** Ritual scroll or slim chapbook. Item = full ritual card.

### G3. Remote Sense [B43]
**(General, Mag 2, Reach)**

- **Does:** Threads the leader’s perception through a spotter — Overlay / Jacked ally, tagged camera, or minor spirit eye. One scene of borrowed senses within printed Reach/Wired range.
- **Duration:** One scene after sealing (or until the link is cut).
- **Impact:** Scouting without bodies in the room; conspiracy table candy. Failed seal or contested trace can reverse the pipe for a round.
- **Combat:** N/A as a lingering summon — if knives come out mid-scene, the link is still a perception channel, not saber-rattling. Spotter may take a narrative jolt on a low prior seal (already paid as Price).
- **Components:** Spotter consent or compromised feed; chalk-link or graph-pin (shoppable); optionally a hair/wire remnant for aim (see G9–G11 if no live spotter).
- **Counter:** Trace and spoof; Unmaking Mag 2 on the seal-mark; kill/knock the spotter.
- **Formula:** Datachip (Wire-native) or scroll. Item = full ritual card.

### G4. Watcher [B43]
**(General, Mag 2, Calling)**

- **Does:** Calls a minor watcher-spirit, elemental extension, or Wire daemon-watcher to guard a marked site. It screams once when the trip condition hits — then waits for orders or fades when hours burn out.
- **Duration:** Hours equal to **4 × Persona** (Priest) / **4 × Logic** (Elem / Techno skin), or until dismissed.
- **Impact:** Alarm clock on a door, alley, or nest. Fiction pressure: ignore the scream → spirit-attention +Mag.
- **Combat:** Default = **alert fiction or one free notice**, not a full combat summon. If upgraded by a later Working or Director call, treat as §C3 **extension** (convenience pool only; prefer untargetable). No resource drip to keep the alert up.
- **Components:** Marking chalk/ash (shoppable); site claim night; optional bait-offering (shoppable or fetch if spirit is picky).
- **Counter:** Banish / Unmaking Mag 2; kill the watcher in a scene if it was upgraded to a figure.
- **Formula:** Ritual scroll; Wire skin as datachip. Item = full ritual card.

### G5. Site Bind [B43]
**(General, Mag 4, Calling)**

- **Does:** Binds a **named** spirit, elemental, or Wire-entity as permanent **site guardian** tied to a sanctum rating 4+. The entity knows the floorplan in its bones. This is a crew/world asset, not a pocket pet.
- **Duration:** While sanctum keystone and favor-debt hold. Slipped leash feeds Incursion/Malice.
- **Impact:** Safehouse with teeth; faction notice at Mag 4. Requires prior Lore or an encounter that named the entity.
- **Combat:** Guardian uses **§C3** Stamina floor (B53 summon scaling as floor). Prefer **independent** pool when it fights on-site; binder controls it as a site NPC asset. **Not** free encounter-summon spam — if the crew is elsewhere, it stays home unless the rite’s card says otherwise. Prefer no Essence/Conviction/Resonance to maintain; ongoing cost is favor/debt / spirit-attention.
- **Components:** Named entity’s true remnant or prior Call; sanctum keystone materials (Restricted+/quest); binding cord or Wire collar-glyph (often **mission fetch**).
- **Counter:** Break sanctum keystone; Unmaking Mag 4 contested with binder.
- **Formula:** Thick tome / bound volume (bulky). Item = full ritual card.

### G6. Circle of Many
**(General, Mag 2–4, Other)**

- **Does:** Formal multi-caster circle — assistants become sealing edges and Project Points. Huge circles may beg the Director for +1 Mag ceiling for one seal.
- **Duration:** For the sealing night / Project block it supports. The circle marks may linger as fiction until cleaned.
- **Impact:** Turns a crew into a ritual machine; non-casters matter. Heat scales with Mag and headcount.
- **Combat:** N/A — downtime / climax-seal beat. Mid-seal interrupt = backlash + components lost + Malice tick (B43).
- **Components:** Circle chalk/ink per station (shoppable); optional resonance capacitors / prayer beads for edges (shoppable); unique “center spike” sometimes **fetch**.
- **Formula:** Scroll (instructions) or lodge tome chapter. Item = full ritual card (includes assistant rules).

### G7. Exclusive Charter Rite
**(General, Mag 3, Other)**

- **Does:** Locks a Working (or a small slate of Workings) so only charter members may lead or seal. Outsiders take a sealing bane; copies “forget” themselves for non-members.
- **Duration:** Permanent until charter is broken or Unmade.
- **Impact:** Cabal identity as mechanics. Theft of formulas becomes incomplete without membership.
- **Combat:** N/A — meta-asset on who can run rites. Does not spawn tokens.
- **Components:** Charter blood/handle ink (fetch or session scene); lodge seal blank (shoppable Restricted); witness spirits or Wire notary daemon (often **favor debt**).
- **Counter:** Unmaking Mag 3+; kill the charter’s living keys (printed).
- **Formula:** Bound volume (charter book). Item = full ritual card + member list fields.

### G8. Lodge Keystone
**(General, Mag 2–3, Ward)**

- **Does:** Raises a shared lodge / group workspace as a permanent sanctum asset — soft +1 sanctum rating (cap Mag 3 without further Projects unless Director opens the ceiling).
- **Duration:** Permanent with lifestyle/upkeep ¥.
- **Impact:** Crew safehouse becomes a real Working floor. Fiction: neighbors notice weird hours.
- **Combat:** Ambient ward-lite on the lodge interior (bane on hostile spirits / Mag peeks) if the card says so; otherwise pure sanctum rating. No caster drip.
- **Components:** Masonry / server-rack / shrine guts (shoppable + construction Project); keystone relic often **mission fetch**.
- **Counter:** Destroy keystone; Unmaking Mag = lodge rating.
- **Formula:** Tome (lodge prospectus) or engraved plaque + chip backup. Item = full ritual card.

### G9. Hair-and-Wire Link
**(General, Mag 2–3, Reach)**

- **Does:** Aims a Reach Working without a live spotter if you hold a **strong remnant** — tissue, blood, god-file shard, deep handle core. Cold, intimate, illegal in half the Reach.
- **Duration:** Empowers one subsequent Reach seal / scene as printed on the card.
- **Impact:** Assassination scouting, missing-person pulls, corp executive hauntings. Louder trace than live-spotter Remote Sense.
- **Combat:** N/A as summon. If used to open a combat-round peek, still perception-only unless chained to an Unmaking strike rite (separate).
- **Components:** Strong remnant (**quest/loot** — almost never shoppable); link-bath salts or graph solvent (shoppable).
- **Counter:** Destroy remnant; false remnant fouls the seal (bane / backlash).
- **Formula:** Scroll in a shielded tube, or encrypted chip. Item = full ritual card.

### G10. Keepsake Link
**(General, Mag 3, Reach)**

- **Does:** Same remote aim via a beloved object or personal archive — weaker sympathy than flesh/wire, harder seal, louder emotional trace.
- **Duration:** One Reach payoff scene as printed.
- **Impact:** Soft entry when you can’t get blood; romance and revenge both leave keepsakes.
- **Combat:** N/A — downtime / scene tool.
- **Components:** Keepsake (**fetch** from target’s life); binder thread (shoppable).
- **Counter:** Return/destroy keepsake; Unmaking Mag 3 on the link-mark.
- **Formula:** Scroll or slim sentimental booklet. Item = full ritual card.

### G11. Effigy Link
**(General, Mag 3–4, Reach)**

- **Does:** Built symbolic stand-in — doll, model flat, glyph-double — aims Reach or Unmaking across distance. Ugly craft. Works when remnants and keepsakes fail.
- **Duration:** Until the effigy is spent on a seal or destroyed.
- **Impact:** High Mag remote strike/sense scaffolding; faction clocks notice Mag 4 builds.
- **Combat:** Effigy itself is an object — destroying it mid-fight collapses any active link. Not a combat summon.
- **Components:** Craft materials (shoppable); true detail tokens (**fetch** — photos, badges, chrome serials); optional forbidden clay (Restricted+/quest).
- **Counter:** Burn the effigy; Unmaking Mag ≥ link Mag.
- **Formula:** Tome with pattern plates, or chip + fab instructions. Item = full ritual card.

### G12. Door-Seal Glyph
**(General, Mag 2, Ward)**

- **Does:** One doorway refuses spirits, Mag peeks, and Wire bleed until broken. Flesh and bullets still negotiate with wood and hinges.
- **Duration:** Until broken, Unmade, or printed expiry (often dawn×Mag or until sanctum upkeep lapses).
- **Impact:** Chokepoint security for flats, vaults, shrine doors.
- **Combat:** Ambient bane / refusal on Veil verbs through that doorway. No resource drip. Breachers can still kick the physical door.
- **Components:** Glyph paint or etch-acid (shoppable); door must be claimed for a sealing hour.
- **Counter:** Scrape the glyph; Unmaking Mag 2; physical destroy + rescrub.
- **Formula:** Scroll or stencil-chip. Item = full ritual card.

### G13. Charged Perimeter
**(General, Mag 3, Ward)**

- **Does:** Quiet ward that stores potency and spends it on breach — alarm scream, stun pulse, and/or printed Malice tick when crossed by the wrong kind of thing.
- **Duration:** Until discharged once, or maintained with sanctum upkeep (¥ / reagents, not pools).
- **Impact:** Trap-ward for galleries, nests, corp ritual floors. Detection clock louder than Ward the Room.
- **Combat:** On breach trigger: printed pulse/alarm. Ambient until then. No caster turn-tax. Re-arming is a downtime Working, not an in-fight spend.
- **Components:** Capacitor salts / prayer batteries / ICE capacitors (shoppable Restricted); breach-condition fetish (**sometimes fetch**).
- **Counter:** Discharge safely with Counterseal tools; Unmaking Mag 3; sacrifice a runner to trip it (fiction).
- **Formula:** Tome section or engineer’s chip. Item = full ritual card.

### G14. Reagent Still
**(General, Mag 1–2, Artifice)**

- **Does:** Alchemy-style prep craft. Distills a **one-use reagent pack** that later grants a sealing edge or blunts Price once on another Working.
- **Duration:** Pack lasts until used or spoils (printed — often one season / three sessions).
- **Impact:** Prep economy. Makes future Mag 3+ seals less suicidal.
- **Combat:** N/A — downtime craft. Pack is gear; using it is a sealing-stage consume, not a combat action.
- **Components:** Base solvents (shoppable); one uncommon flower/mineral/code-seed (**often fetch**); still access (lodge or rented lab).
- **Formula:** Scroll recipe or chip. Item = full ritual card; output may be a separate consumable Item.

### G15. True Relic Carve
**(General, Mag 4–5, Artifice)**

- **Does:** Carves a **permanent prototype relic** (Restricted+). Unique flags, Director veto, faction clocks. This is campaign gravity, not a shop SKU.
- **Duration:** Permanent until destroyed / Unbonded / corrupted.
- **Impact:** Changes what the crew can attempt. Mag 5 = hive-wide whisper.
- **Combat:** Gear flags only. No upkeep pool spend. If the relic has a Persistent Mag ability, that ability’s **class** Persistent math applies — not a ritual tax.
- **Components:** Prototype blank (**mission fetch** almost always); master tools; named spirit/Wire blessing (favor); Availability gates stacked.
- **Counter:** Destroy; Unbond Mag 4–5 Project; printed relic doom clock.
- **Formula:** Thick grimoire / fab-tome (bulky). Item = full ritual card.

### G16. Long Leash
**(General, Mag 3–4, Calling)**

- **Does:** Long-term binding of an **already-called** entity for intermittent **services over days/weeks** — courier, watch, whisper, one dirty job — not “wins this fight.”
- **Duration:** Days to weeks as sealed; renew with favor or lapse.
- **Impact:** Downtime labor and intel. Relationship sheet with a not-human.
- **Combat:** If present when knives come out: §C3 **extension or independent** per card (default extension / site helper). Prefer no pool spend to keep. Ritual leash ≠ encounter Summon Elemental spam; bind count courtesy — don’t stack past §C3 soft caps without Director OK.
- **Components:** Prior successful Call or capture; leash cord / daemon schedule chip (shoppable Restricted); feeding terms (**fetch** offerings sometimes).
- **Counter:** Banish; break schedule tablet; Unmaking Mag 3–4.
- **Formula:** Bound service-contract tome or encrypted chip. Item = full ritual card.

### G17. True-Name Collar (Deep Bind)
**(General, Mag 4–5, Calling)**

- **Does:** Collars an **unbound named power** via secret name / deep handle. High Price, printed escape conditions. This is the catalog’s **Deep Bind** — hubris with a warranty void sticker.
- **Duration:** Until escape condition, Unmaking, or binder’s doom.
- **Impact:** Campaign villain-or-ally on a string. Mag 5 seals move faction clocks whether you like it or not.
- **Combat:** When forced into a fight: §C3 **independent** Stamina (scale to named power — Director, floor at Rank 3+/spirit independent). Binder controls only within printed collar terms; escape mid-fight is a feature. Prefer no Essence/Conviction/Resonance maintain.
- **Components:** True name / handle (**major quest**); collar forge materials (Restricted+); witness circle (G6 often required).
- **Counter:** Printed escape; Unmaking Mag 4–5 contested; destroy name-vessel.
- **Formula:** Heavy grimoire (bulky) + sealed name-folio. Item = full ritual card (escape conditions player-visible or secret-blocked per Director taste).

### G18. Vessel Dressing
**(General, Mag 3, Calling)**

- **Does:** Preps a body, drone shell, or object as a **legal vessel** for a called entity to wear. Without this, inhabitation is messy, hostile, or lethal to the meat.
- **Duration:** Prep holds until inhabited or spoils (printed — often days×Mag).
- **Impact:** Enables clean possession / drone-ride / idol-walk for later Calling payoffs.
- **Combat:** Empty dressed vessel is gear/prop. Once inhabited, use §C3 for the rider; vessel may have machine Stamina if it’s a drone (Machines chapter). No ritual pool tax.
- **Components:** Vessel (owned / stolen); dressing oils, etch, or Wire permission-glyphs (shoppable + often **chrome serial fetch**); consent or corpse ethics (session heat).
- **Counter:** Spoil dressing with Unmaking Mag 3; wreck the vessel.
- **Formula:** Manual tome or tech-chip. Item = full ritual card.

### G19. Great Circle
**(General, Mag 4–5, Other)**

- **Does:** Great-ritual-scale ceiling break — one sealing may exceed Mag cap by **+1** with initiate-grade leader + full circle. Campaign lever; easy to regret.
- **Duration:** Empowers a single sealing night.
- **Impact:** Lets Mag 4 crews kiss Mag 5 payoffs once. Hive-wide detection.
- **Combat:** N/A — climax ritual beat. Interrupt is catastrophic (Malice + backlash).
- **Components:** Initiate credentials (**quest**); full circle stations; rare sky-ash or deep-node spike (**mission fetch**).
- **Formula:** Restricted grimoire (bulky). Item = full ritual card + Director veto callout.

### G20. Scrub the Stain
**(General, Mag ≥ original, Unmaking)**

- **Does:** Strips a hung curse, toxic mark, or corrupted ward residue. Magnitude must meet or beat the stain’s Mag.
- **Duration:** Instant at successful seal; stain gone or reduced one clock step on partial.
- **Impact:** Cleans crew, site, or ally. Failure can smear the scrubber.
- **Combat:** N/A — downtime / dedicated beat. If scrubbing mid-siege, still a Project seal, not an encounter ability.
- **Components:** Solvents matched to stain type (shoppable + specialty); sample of the stain (**fetch**); sacrificial scrub-sink (lodge).
- **Counter:** N/A (you are the counter). Rival can re-stain.
- **Formula:** Scroll or chip annotated with stain taxonomy. Item = full ritual card.

## 2. Elementalist only

### E1. Thin-Path [B43]
**(Elementalist only, Mag 3, Threshold)**

- **Does:** Opens a short path between two **known** thin places for one crew-sized traversal. Closes behind unless Mag 4+ with permanent sanctum (not this card’s default).
- **Duration:** One traversal; path collapses after.
- **Impact:** Ghost logistics. Automatic district notice; Concord/corp clocks on Mag 3+ Threshold in the Reach.
- **Combat:** N/A as lingering summon — if ambushed mid-crossing, fight is on the path (Director map). Traveler tolls already paid as Price (Stamina or recovery debt).
- **Components:** Maps of both thin places (Lore); path-spikes (Restricted shoppable or **fetch**); traveler offerings.
- **Counter:** Collapse from either end (Unmaking Mag 3+); destroy either anchor.
- **Formula:** Weather-stained tome or scroll tube. Item = full ritual card.

### E2. Domain Aspect
**(Elementalist only, Mag 3–4, Threshold)**

- **Does:** Paints session-long **Veil weather** on a block — edges/banes + fiction tint (ash rain, static bloom, soft gravity). Your tradition’s favorite season, weaponized as climate.
- **Duration:** One session (or until dawn×2 — card picks).
- **Impact:** Home-turf advantage; civilians feel it; opposition plans around it.
- **Combat:** Ambient edges/banes on the block for matching / opposing elements and spirits. No caster drip to maintain the weather.
- **Components:** Domain pigment / stormglass (shoppable Restricted); block claim walk; optional stolen weather-vane from a thin place (**fetch**).
- **Counter:** Opposing Domain Aspect; Unmaking Mag 3–4; wait it out.
- **Formula:** Elemental almanac tome. Item = full ritual card.

### E3. Invoke Greater Shape
**(Elementalist only, Mag 4–5, Calling)**

- **Does:** Calls a **greater-shape** elemental for one climax scene. Price + Malice spike. This is the “we brought a disaster on a leash” button.
- **Duration:** One climax scene / encounter; then gone or unbound violently.
- **Impact:** Set-piece summon. Not a downtime butler (contrast Long Leash).
- **Combat:** §C3 Rank **4** (or Rank **5** at Mag 5 / L10 floor per Greater Elemental Summon numbers): independent Stamina **50 or 65 + Logic × level**. Leader commands per elemental bind rules. Prefer no extra ritual Persistent; if class Greater Summon Persistent exists, only use that math if the card explicitly says the rite piggybacks it.
- **Components:** Greater true-sign (**quest**); massive reagent burn (¥ band high); open sky or deep basement sanctum.
- **Counter:** Banish contested; Unmaking Mag 4–5; let the clock run out.
- **Formula:** Heavy grimoire (bulky). Item = full ritual card + §C3 stamp reference.

### E4. Anchor Rune
**(Elementalist only, Mag 3, Artifice)**

- **Does:** Anchors a lasting **triggered** rune that auto-fires one known Mag ability on a printed trigger, then is spent.
- **Duration:** Until trigger or spoil (often session or until next respite).
- **Impact:** Trap, deadman, or “when the door opens, wall of fire.”
- **Combat:** Rune is environment gear. When it fires, resolve the named ability **once** without the caster spending Essence for that shot (already paid in ritual). No ongoing drip.
- **Components:** Rune blank (shoppable); sample of the ability’s tradition stain (**fetch** if exotic); anchor nail.
- **Counter:** Scrape rune; Disarm scene; Unmaking Mag 3.
- **Formula:** Scroll with trigger diagrams. Item = full ritual card.

### E5. Telesma Birth
**(Elementalist only, Mag 2–3, Artifice)**

- **Does:** Creates or upgrades a **tradition focus** from prepared telesma — not merely charming an existing stick (contrast Street-Focus Charm).
- **Duration:** Permanent focus Item until destroyed.
- **Impact:** Builds the Elementalist’s toolkit between echelons.
- **Combat:** Gear flags. No upkeep pool.
- **Components:** Telesma blank (Restricted shoppable or craft Project); elemental heart-matter (**often fetch**); binding wire.
- **Counter:** Shatter focus; Unbond Mag 2–3.
- **Formula:** Craft tome or scroll. Item = full ritual card; output is a separate focus Item.

### E6. Storm Ward
**(Elementalist only, Mag 2–3, Ward)**

- **Does:** Element-aspected barrier that especially punishes the **opposite** element/spirit type. Your fire-ward hates water ghosts; your stone-ward hates sky things.
- **Duration:** Printed — dawn, session, or sanctum upkeep.
- **Impact:** Selective defense for elemental turf wars.
- **Combat:** Ambient banes/edges vs opposed types in the warded zone. No caster drip.
- **Components:** Aspect salts (shoppable); opposing-type trophy (**fetch** strengthens); circle time.
- **Counter:** Matching Unmaking; opposite Domain Aspect; break circle.
- **Formula:** Scroll or chapbook. Item = full ritual card.

### E7. Nest Claim *(catalog adjacent — nest / watcher upgrade path)*
**(Elementalist only, Mag 3, Calling)** — *Director optional row if nesting fiction is needed; else treat as Watcher upgrade clause on G4.*

- **Does:** Claims a vermin/elemental **nest** as a living alarm lattice across a small site — multiple tiny watchers as one Working.
- **Duration:** Days equal to Logic, or until nest burned.
- **Impact:** Block- sensitve early warning; civilians complain about “bugs that stare.”
- **Combat:** Alert fiction / free notices — **not** a swarm summon unless Hive Crack aftermath says so. Upgraded fight = many extensions, still no pool drip.
- **Components:** Queen-mote or nest seed (**fetch**); feeding slurry (shoppable).
- **Formula:** Field notebook scroll. Item = full ritual card.

### E8. Hive Crack
**(Elementalist only — threat extra, Mag 4–5, Unmaking)**

- **Does:** Collapses a nest-sanctum keystone opposing hive/infestation agendas. Swarm encounter is a common Price.
- **Duration:** Instant structural/spiritual collapse at seal; aftermath clocks linger.
- **Impact:** Anti-infestation campaign tool; expect a fight as payment.
- **Combat:** Price may **be** a swarm combat. The Working itself isn’t maintained in the fight — you already lit the fuse.
- **Components:** Keystone map (**quest**); solvent bomb reagents (Restricted); bait-sacrifice optional (heat).
- **Formula:** Restricted grimoire or scavenged chip from a dead exterminator-cabal. Item = full ritual card.

## 3. Street Priest only

### P1. Consecrated Thin-Path
**(Street Priest only, Mag 3, Threshold)**

### P2. Pact Charter
**(Street Priest only, Mag 2–3, Other)**

- **Does:** Binds a crew to **Light or Dark terms**. Break → backlash; keep → sealing edges on later Workings.
- **Duration:** Until broken, rewritten, or Unmade.
- **Impact:** Crew identity as rules. Roleplay fuel with teeth.
- **Combat:** N/A as summon. Broken terms mid-fight may trigger printed backlash (Director) — still not a Conviction tax to “maintain” the charter.
- **Components:** Pact ink (shoppable Restricted); crew signatures / blood; ministry co-sign (**favor** often).
- **Counter:** Exorcist’s Unmaking variant; mutual release scene; rival corruption.
- **Formula:** Bound charter volume (bulky). Item = full ritual card + terms list.

### P3. Ally Heart
**(Street Priest only, Mag 4–5, Calling)**

- **Does:** Forges a long-term **ally companion spirit** with a short power list and ongoing favor debt. Not a disposable Watcher.
- **Duration:** Campaign-long while debt is honored.
- **Impact:** Named ally on the crew sheet. Stories stick to them.
- **Combat:** §C3 spirit math — default **independent** **30 + Persona × level** when they take the field; extension if the card says “whisper only.” Prefer no Conviction drip to keep; favor debt is the upkeep. Does not replace Invoke the Pact encounter uses without Director bridge.
- **Components:** Heart-seed relic (**major fetch**); pact witness; gift of memory or chrome.
- **Counter:** Ally Reforge fail; Unmaking Mag 4–5; betray the terms.
- **Formula:** Thick hagiography tome (bulky). Item = full ritual card + ally power slots.

### P4. Ally Reforge
**(Street Priest only, Mag 3–4, Calling)**

- **Does:** Adds or swaps one ally power. Failure **fractures** the bond (ally hurt, fled, or hostile for a clock).
- **Duration:** Instant change; ally duration unchanged.
- **Impact:** Build-your-ally between arcs. High risk mid-campaign.
- **Combat:** N/A — downtime. Fractured ally may become a combat problem later.
- **Components:** Existing Ally Heart; rewrite ink; trial offering (**fetch** matching new power).
- **Counter:** N/A; failure is the counter.
- **Formula:** Addendum scroll bound into the Ally Heart tome. Item = full ritual card.

### P5. Exorcist’s Unmaking
**(Street Priest only, Mag 3–4, Unmaking)**

- **Does:** Tears a possessing entity from vessel or site. Vessel may not survive. This is the kind door with a boot behind it.
- **Duration:** Instant at seal; possession ends or worsens on fail.
- **Impact:** Saves (or martyrs) the ridden. Site cleansings for haunted blocks.
- **Combat:** Often a dedicated climax scene with the possessor as enemy. Use §C3 for the entity while it’s being pried out. Ritual does not spend Conviction; priest may still use encounter abilities normally in that fight.
- **Components:** Bell, book, and street-compatible third (shoppable + holy/unholy fetch); true name helps (G17 remnant).
- **Counter:** Possessor’s rival ward; interrupt seal.
- **Formula:** Exorcism manual tome or scrolled rite. Item = full ritual card.

### P6. Blood-Price Refusal
**(Street Priest only, Mag 3–5, Unmaking)**

- **Does:** Spoils a rival **sacrifice-fueled** sealing mid-agenda. High heat. You are kicking over someone else’s altar during the hymn.
- **Duration:** Spoils one rival Working / clock step.
- **Impact:** Anti-blood-agenda tool; makes enemies forever.
- **Combat:** Frequently lands as a raid climax — fight uses normal pools; the Refusal seal is the Project beat before or between knives.
- **Components:** Rival rite sample (**mission fetch**); refusal nail; Light or Dark counter-offering (shoppable Restricted).
- **Counter:** Rival Counterseal; being out-Mag’d.
- **Formula:** Proscribed scroll (dangerous to carry) or sealed tome. Item = full ritual card.

### P7. Confession Ward
**(Street Priest only, Mag 1–2, Ward)**

- **Does:** Faith/pact-keyed barrier. Allies of the pact pass easier; opposed take a bane. Soft door policy with teeth.
- **Duration:** Dawn or sanctum upkeep.
- **Impact:** Ministry safehouse filter; identifies liars who flinch at the threshold (fiction).
- **Combat:** Ambient edges/banes by pact tag. No Conviction drip.
- **Components:** Confession salt or ash (shoppable); pact mark on the lintel.
- **Counter:** Desecrate; Unmaking Mag 1–2; false confession trick (Director).
- **Formula:** Prayer scroll. Item = full ritual card.

### P8. Uncrook the Path
**(Street Priest only — threat extra, Mag 3–5, Unmaking)**

- **Does:** Breaks a rival **twisted/corrupted tradition** Working. May reveal the true patron behind the crooked ministry.
- **Duration:** Instant vs target Working; revelation clocks linger.
- **Impact:** Campaign scalpel against dark mirrors of your own faith.
- **Combat:** N/A maintain — spoil beat may trigger guardian fight afterward.
- **Components:** Proof of twist (**investigation fetch**); straight-path relic; Mag ≥ target.
- **Formula:** Inquisitorial tome (bulky) or deniable chip. Item = full ritual card.

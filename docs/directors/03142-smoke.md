# 0.3.142 Foundry checklist — Biofeedback, ICE triggers, North Substation, VOIDMARK

Offline asserts: `node tools/wave-03142-smoke.mjs`. This page is the part a machine cannot check —
what to press in a live world, and what the right answer looks like.

**Setup.** A world on the stock `draw-steel` system with **Draw Steel – Ghostwire Build 0.3.142**
enabled. One hero Actor you are happy to damage (a Hacker pregen such as **Vira** is ideal — she
carries a deck). Log in as **Director / GM**.

---

## 1. The macro exists and is Director-only

1. Open **Compendiums → Ghostwire Macros**. **Director: Apply Biofeedback** is in the list, beside
   *Director: Pay Hero* and *Director: Spend Hero*, on a blue lightning-bolt icon.
2. There is **no new Token toolbar button** — the 0.3.137 declutter still holds. The tool is the macro
   plus the (unbound) keybinding under **Configure Controls → Draw Steel – Ghostwire Build**.
3. Log in as a **player** and run the macro from a hotbar slot. Expect a warning
   *"Only the Director can apply Biofeedback"* and **no change** to any Stamina.

**Pass:** macro present, no toolbar button, players refused.

## 2. Auto-detect: connection state and deck resistance

1. As Director, put your hero's token on a scene. Leave them **Disconnected**.
2. Target the token and run **Director: Apply Biofeedback**. The dialog's target line reads
   *`<hero>` — Disconnected — no Biofeedback, resistance N*, and the **Connection state** dropdown
   says **Auto — Disconnected**.
3. Cancel. Use **Cycle Wire State** (or the node applet's Connect) to put them in **Linked**, then
   **Overlay**, then **Jacked In**, reopening the dialog each time. The **Auto —** label follows the
   status every time.
4. With a hero carrying a **Street Deck**, the **Biofeedback Resistance** field shows
   **Auto — 2** at Echelon 1. Strip the deck off the sheet and it shows **Auto — 0**.

**Pass:** the dialog reads the live state and the carried deck, with no typing.

## 3. The four steps, printed in chat

1. Put the hero in **Overlay**. Note their Stamina.
2. Run the macro: **Host Node Rating 3**, custom base blank, state **Auto**, resistance **Auto**,
   trigger *Low (≤11) Wired Power Roll vs active ICE (R3+)*. Apply.
3. The chat card shows, in order:
   - `1. Base: Rating 3 host, Biofeedback Value 8`
   - `2. Overlay — ×0.5, round down, min 1 → 4`
   - `3. Less Biofeedback Resistance 2 → 2 damage`
   - `4. Stamina <before> → <after>`
4. The hero's Stamina on the sheet and the token bar dropped by exactly **2**.
5. Now put them **Jacked In** and repeat with the same Rating 3. The card reads `→ 12`, then
   `→ 10 damage`, and Stamina drops by **10**.

**Pass:** 8 → 4 → 2 Overlaid, 8 → 12 → 10 Jacked In, both written through to the Actor.

## 4. Linked and Disconnected take nothing

1. Put the hero in **Linked**. Run the macro at **Rating 5** (base 22), resistance **Auto**.
2. The card reads `2. Linked — no Biofeedback → 0` and
   `No Biofeedback lands: the scale or the resistance took it to zero.` **Stamina does not move.**
3. Repeat **Disconnected**. Same answer.

**Pass:** a Rating 5 host cannot touch a Linked or Disconnected runner.

## 5. The Winded floor

1. Put the hero **Jacked In** and edit their Stamina down to **3**.
2. Run the macro at **Rating 3** (which is 10 damage after a resistance of 2).
3. Stamina lands on **1**, not on −7. The card adds
   *"Winded floor: Biofeedback stopped at 1 Stamina. `<hero>` is Winded, not Dying — but a bullet still
   kills."* The notification says the same.
4. Run it **again** at the same settings. Stamina **stays at 1** — the floor never heals and never
   pushes past itself.
5. Now damage the hero by ordinary means (a strike, or editing Stamina to 0). They go to **0 and
   Dying** as normal: the floor covers Biofeedback only.

**Pass:** held at 1, Winded not Dying, idempotent, and non-Biofeedback damage still kills.

## 6. The rules say the same thing as the macro

1. Open **Compendiums → Ghostwire Rulebook → Ghostwire Systems → The Wired System**.
2. On the **The Wired System** page, find **Biofeedback -- the full procedure**: four numbered steps,
   the worked line `8 × 0.5 = 4 → minus 2 → 2 Stamina damage`, and **The Winded floor (both sources)`.
3. Just below it, **ICE attack triggers -- when ICE actually bites** lists **four** triggers and opens
   with *"ICE does not get a free attack every round."* Passive R1–2 is flavour, Linked takes none,
   host ICE bites the compiler, and a Black ICE *creature* is called out as a different thing.
4. In the **Trace Alert** table, the **9–11** row now delivers the **hunt bite** (one Biofeedback hit
   at the end of each of the runner's turns while Overlay or Jacked In) rather than a bane.
5. Open **Classes → The Technomancer → Biofeedback — The Cost of Overreach**. The Winded floor is
   worded the same way as the Wire chapter and says it is the same floor. The 5+ Resonance Physique
   test by cost band is unchanged.
6. Open **The Hacker → Cyberdecks (Your Kit)**. Biofeedback Resistance is described as per-Echelon,
   applied after the connection scale, and the five Matrix deck numbers are printed
   (Scrapdeck 1 · Street Deck 2 · Ghostbox 2 · Blackdeck 3 · Fairlight Ghost 3).
7. Both class **Wired firefight** checklists carry *"ICE does not attack every round"* and the four
   triggers.

**Pass:** no page contradicts the macro, and no second procedure exists anywhere.

## 7. North Substation

1. In the same journal, open the page **North Substation — a worked Wired run**. It runs Act 0 through
   Act 6 plus a Technomancer appendix, with the Rating 3 host (Integrity 26, Biofeedback 8, Alert 0),
   Vira's street deck at resistance 2, and Kade as the meat half.
2. Act 2's bite is **2**; Act 4's Malice surge is **10**. These are the same two numbers the macro
   produced in check 3.
3. Act 3's Special Agent is given a **Wire** purpose (*spoof the turret lock*), and the page names the
   meatspace asks that are refused.
4. Act 6 carries Lock A construct visibility and the Alert persisting past Jack Out.
5. Open `docs/directors/north-substation-masterwork.md` in the repo: the Director cue sheet, the
   act-by-act table, the three things tables get wrong, and the Foundry alignment note.

**Pass:** the worked run reads as one continuous run and its numbers match the macro.

## 8. VOIDMARK

1. Open **VOIDMARK** from the Token controls (ghost icon), in **Director** mode.
2. Ask **"how does biofeedback work?"** The answer gives the four steps with the Rating table, the
   connection scale (Linked 0 · Overlay ×0.5 min 1 · Jacked In ×1.5), the resistance subtraction, and
   the Winded floor. It does not invent a ×1 rung.
3. Ask **"does ICE attack every round?"** The answer is **no**, followed by the four triggers.
4. Ask **"what happens at North Substation?"** It recounts the worked run.
5. Ask **"can my Special Agent pull a downed runner out of a corridor?"** The answer is **no** —
   Agents and sprites are Wire-only — and it offers the Wire version of what you wanted.
6. Switch to **Runner** mode and ask 2 and 5 again. Same rules, in voice, no chapter filenames.

**Pass:** all five answers, no contradiction with the journal, no "that packet is not on this channel".

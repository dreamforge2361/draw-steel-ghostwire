# 0.3.143 Foundry checklist — the node applet's ICE package

Offline asserts: `node tools/wave-03143-smoke.mjs`. This page is the part a machine cannot check —
what to press in a live world, and what the right answer looks like.

**Setup.** A world on the stock `draw-steel` system with **Draw Steel – Ghostwire Build 0.3.143**
enabled. Log in as **Director / GM**. You need:

- one hero you are happy to damage, carrying a **Street Deck** (Biofeedback Resistance **2**) — a
  Hacker pregen such as **Vira** is ideal;
- a scene with a **Wired board**: open the **Wired Console** (network icon in the Token controls) and
  add three nodes — a **Track 2, Rating 3** host, a **Track 2, Rating 4** host, and a **Track 1**
  object (a maglock or a light). **Reveal** all three and **Place on canvas**;
- the hero's token on that scene, and a **Wire Kit** or deck on their sheet so **Connect** works.

Throughout: **nothing in this wave ever writes Stamina without showing you a card first.** If any
step here moves Stamina with no dialog, that is the bug and everything else is secondary.

---

## 1. The reactive low bite

1. Double-click the **Rating 3** node token to open the node applet. Select your hero.
2. **Connect** (you land in **Linked**), then **Toggle Connection State** once (**Overlay**).
3. Fire **Read/Write** (or **Search**, or **Navigate**) at the node, and keep firing until a Power
   Roll comes in at **11 or under**.
4. A dialog opens: **ICE bite — `<hero>`**, headed *"ICE reactive bite on `<hero>`?"*, with a hint
   line naming the node, the fact it has active ICE, and the roll total.
5. The card lists four numbered lines:
   - `1. Base: Rating 3 host, Biofeedback Value 8`
   - `2. Overlay → 4`
   - `3. Less Biofeedback Resistance 2 → 2 damage`
   - `4. Stamina <before> → <after>`
6. Press **Cancel**. **Stamina does not move** and **nothing posts to chat.**
7. Roll another low result and press **Apply 2** this time. Stamina drops by exactly **2**, and the
   ordinary 0.3.142 Biofeedback chat card posts, naming the trigger *Low (≤11) Wired Power Roll vs
   active ICE (R3+)*.
8. **Toggle** to **Jacked In** and repeat. The same Rating 3 host now offers **10**.

**Pass:** a low roll asks, Cancel is free, Apply writes exactly the number on the card.

## 2. Linked never fires, and Rating 2 never fires

1. **Toggle** until the hero is back at **Linked**. Fire **Broadcast** — that works from Linked.
2. Now fire any rollable verb the applet still offers you and roll low. **No card appears**, ever.
   A Linked runner takes no Biofeedback at all, so the applet does not even ask.
3. In the Console, set a node to **Rating 2**, reveal it, go **Overlay** and roll low against it. **No
   card** — passive ICE at Rating 1–2 is flavour.
4. Roll low against the **Track 1** object. **No card** — Track 1 objects carry no ICE.

**Pass:** three different "nothing happens" cases, all silent and all correct.

## 3. The failed breach at Rating 4+

1. Open the **Rating 4** node. Its System Stat Card breach difficulty is **DC 17**.
2. From **Overlay** or **Jacked In**, fire **Read/Write** and land a roll of **12–16** — a middle
   result that still misses the DC.
3. One card opens: *"Failed breach — ICE bites `<hero>`?"*, and its hint names the Rating, the Breach
   **DC 17**, and the roll. The math is the Rating 4 line: base **13**.
4. Now land a roll of **11 or under** on the same Rating 4 host. **Two cards** open, one after the
   other — the low roll and the failed breach are two different triggers, so they are two bites. You
   may Apply both, Cancel both, or Apply one and Cancel the other.
5. Fire **Scan** at the same host and roll low. **No breach card** — Scan is observation, not a
   breach. (A low Scan still gets the trigger-1 card if the host has active ICE.)

**Pass:** DC-aware, and two triggers on one roll really are two cards.

## 4. The Alert strip — 5 / 9 / 12

1. Open the node applet. Under **Trace Alert** the twelve steps now carry a dashed outline on **5**,
   **9** and **12**, and under the bar a three-line legend reads:
   - **5** Malice — the Director banks +1 per step from here.
   - **9** Hunt — ICE bites at the end of each immersed turn.
   - **12** Lockout — counter-trace to your body, then reset to 6.
   Steps already reached are shown in full-strength text.
2. In the **Wired Console**, press the node's Alert **+** until the track crosses **5**. Chat posts a
   **Trace Alert** card naming the node and the +1 Malice, and a notification says the same.
3. Keep going to **9**. A second announcement: active ICE is hunting.
4. Press **Max** (12). A third: full lockout and a hard counter-trace, then reset to 6.
5. Press **Reset**. The track drops to 6 and **nothing is announced** — coming down is bookkeeping.
6. Set the Alert from **4 straight to 9** in one move (type it, or use Max then Reset then +). **Both**
   announcements post, 5 first and then 9.

**Pass:** upward crossings announce once each, downward announce nothing.

## 5. The hunt bite at the end of a turn

1. Set the **Rating 3** node's Trace Alert to **9**. The node applet shows a red notice: *"`<node>` is
   hunting at Alert 9…"*.
2. Put the hero **Overlay** (or Jacked In) and start a **combat encounter** with them in it.
3. End the hero's turn (advance the tracker). A card opens: *"Hunt bite on `<hero>`?"*, with the hint
   naming the node and the Alert, and the same four-line math.
4. **Cancel.** Advance the turn again so the hero ends another turn: the card offers again. It offers
   **once per turn**, not once per click — re-rendering the tracker does not stack cards.
5. **Apply** on a later turn and let Stamina run down. When a bite would take them to 0 or below, the
   card's math ends with *"Winded floor: this stops at 1 Stamina…"* and the hero holds at **1**.
6. **Toggle** the hero back to **Linked** and end their turn. **No card.** **Jack Out** and end
   another turn. **No card.** Set the Alert to **12** and end a turn: **no card** either — 12 is
   lockout, which is its own row, not the hunt band.

**Pass:** one bite per ended turn while immersed at 9–11, and nothing otherwise.

## 6. The Malice ICE surge button

1. On the **Rating 3** node applet (as Director), a **Malice ICE surge** button sits under the Alert
   strip. Hover it: the tooltip names your current Malice and says Ghostwire does not spend it.
2. It is **not** on the Track 1 object's applet, and a **player** does not see it at all.
3. Press it. If more than one runner on the scene is Overlay or Jacked In, a small picker opens with
   the compiler preselected. Choose and press **Continue**.
4. The usual confirm card opens, headed *"Malice ICE surge on `<hero>`?"*. **Cancel** — nothing
   happens, and no chat posts.
5. Press it again and **Apply**. Stamina moves by the card's number, the 0.3.142 Biofeedback card
   posts with the trigger named *Director Malice ICE surge*, and a second short card says
   **Director: Malice ICE surge** and reminds you to adjust Malice yourself.
6. **Check your Malice count in the Draw Steel UI: it has not changed.** That is deliberate.
7. Put every runner to **Linked** and press the button. You get *"Nobody on this scene is Overlay or
   Jacked In…"* and no card.

**Pass:** Director-only, target-picked, confirmed, and your Malice is untouched.

## 7. Soft refuses — why, and what next

1. Put the hero **Linked**. On the node applet, hover the greyed-out **Scan** button. The tooltip now
   reads the rule *and* the next step: *"Linked is soft presence, not immersion… Scan requires Overlay
   or Jacked In — Toggle Connection State first."*
2. Click it anyway (from the Director Console strip, or by firing the verb). A **Refused** card is
   whispered to you with two lines: the **why**, then the **hint**. The verb does not roll and costs
   nothing.
3. **Disconnected** → the hint points at **Connect**. **Already on-net** → it points at **Toggle
   Connection State**. **No deck or commlink** → it tells you to put one on the sheet.
4. Go **Overlay** and fire **Ping** at the **Track 2** host. It refuses: *"Ping is a nudge at a simple
   Track 1 object. This is a Track 2 host, which is where the ICE lives, and Ping never opens or
   defeats ICE."* The hint points at **Read/Write**, a Program, or a Track 1 target.
5. Fire **Ping** at the **Track 1** object. It works normally.

**Pass:** every refuse answers "why not?" and "what instead?", and Ping knows its Track.

## 8. The Wire run checklist

1. On the node applet, below the Alert strip, a collapsed row reads **Wire run checklist**. Click it.
2. Eight steps open in North Substation order, each with a one-line cue:
   Connect → Linked → Broadcast · Toggle to Overlay · Scan / Deep Scan · Track 1 vs Track 2 ·
   Watch lows against ICE · Integrity · Alert bands 1–4 / 5–8 / 9–11 / 12 · Decompile / Jack Out.
3. Tick two steps. They turn green. Click one again — it clears.
4. Close and reopen the applet, and open a **different** node: the open/closed state follows you, and
   the **ticks are per node**.
5. Log in as a **player**: they get their own checklist and their own ticks. Nothing you tick shows up
   for them, and nothing they tick shows up for you.
6. **Ticking a step changes nothing else.** With every box ticked, the verbs gate exactly as before;
   with none ticked, every verb you could fire before still fires.

**Pass:** guidance that blocks nothing, stored per user.

## 9. The rules and VOIDMARK agree

1. Open **Compendiums → Ghostwire Rulebook → Ghostwire Systems → The Wired System**, and find
   **ICE attack triggers -- when ICE actually bites**. Below the four triggers there is a new
   *In Foundry* box describing exactly what you just pressed.
2. It says **nothing is ever applied silently**, that **Cancel leaves Stamina exactly where it is**,
   and that Ghostwire **does not spend your Malice**.
3. Open **VOIDMARK** (ghost icon) in **Director** mode and ask *"does the node applet apply
   biofeedback automatically?"* The answer is **no** — it raises a confirm card with the math
   prefilled — and it does not invent a setting that turns the card off.
4. Ask *"what is the wire run checklist?"* It describes the spine.

**Pass:** the book, the applet and VOIDMARK say one thing.

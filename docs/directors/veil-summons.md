# Veil summons: elementals and pact spirits (Director note)

**Module 0.1.78 · spike `docs/spikes/B53-SUMMON-SCALING-SPIRITS-ELEMENTALS.md`**

When an Elementalist or Street Priest uses a summon ability from their sheet, Ghostwire places the summon beside them. Its Stamina is scaled to the caster:

- **Ember / Zephyr / Boulder Companion:** Rank 1, 15 + Logic × level, 3 rounds.
- **Summon Elemental:** up to Rank 1 (from L1), Rank 2 (from L5) or Rank 3 (from L7). Rank base 15 / 25 / 35 + Logic × level.
- **Twin Elemental Summon:** two binds; one may be independent from echelon 3.
- **Greater Elemental Summon:** Rank 4 (50 + Logic × level), or Rank 5 at L10 (65 + Logic × level).
- **Invoke the Pact:**
  - Low: nothing appears.
  - Middle: the ministry's spirit as an extension, 20 + Persona × level.
  - High: independent, 30 + Persona × level.

  The spirit arrives tinted to the priest's pact.

These numbers are **provisional until the Veil entity table (§C3)**.

**At the table**
- A low Bind roll on an elemental places it **hostile** for one round. It's yours to run.
- Companions and pact spirits end with the encounter; bound elementals stay until dismissed or dropped.
- Levelling up re-stamps live summons. Damage already taken carries over.
- Sustain costs (Persistent Essence / Conviction) are still tracked by hand.
- Prefer to place summons yourself? Turn off **Veil summons: place on ability use** in Configure Settings. The Summon button on each ability sheet still works.
- Players need **Create Actors** and **Create Tokens** permission to summon; otherwise, summon for them from the ability sheet.
